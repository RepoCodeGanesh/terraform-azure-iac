# RBI Master Direction on Outsourcing of Information Technology Services

## 1. Scope & Applicability
Regulates the outsourcing of IT and FinTech services by Scheduled Commercial Banks, Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, NBFCs, and Credit Information Companies.

---

## 2. Core Management Prohibitions

### Section 6.3: Non-Delegable Core Functions
Regulated entities are **strictly prohibited** from outsourcing core management and governance functions:
1. **CISO Oversight & Cybersecurity Policy:** Policy formulation, security risk assessment, and incident sign-off.
2. **Compliance Auditing:** Independent regulatory compliance reviews and statutory reporting to RBI.
3. **Credit Sanctioning & Risk Assessment:** Final credit underwriting, loan approvals, and Know Your Customer (KYC) decision-making.
4. **Senior Management Accountability:** The ultimate responsibility for IT operations and customer data protection remains with the Bank's Board and Senior Management.

---

## 3. Third-Party FinTech Vendor Risk Management

### Section 7.2: Vendor Due Diligence & Audits
* **Audit Certifications:** Third-party FinTech vendors and SaaS providers handling sensitive financial operations must submit to regular **SOC-2 Type II** or **SAS-70 / ISO 27001** independent audit inspections.
* **Continuous Monitoring:** Financial stability, concentration risk, and sub-contracting chains of vendors must be assessed at least **annually**.

### Section 8.4: Sub-Contracting Controls
* Service providers shall not sub-contract any core component of the outsourced IT service without **prior written approval** of the regulated entity.
* Sub-contractors must comply with the same cybersecurity, confidentiality, and data localization standards as the primary vendor.

---

## 4. Business Continuity & Exit Strategies

### Section 12.1: 90-Day Exit & Transition Plan
* Every IT outsourcing agreement must contain an actionable **Exit Strategy and Business Continuity Plan (BCP)** to ensure seamless migration of services and data back to the bank or an alternate vendor within **90 days** without operational disruption.
* Data sanitization and non-disclosure certificates must be obtained upon contract termination.
