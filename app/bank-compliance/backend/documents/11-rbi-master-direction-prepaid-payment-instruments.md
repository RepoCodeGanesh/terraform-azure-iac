# Master Direction on Prepaid Payment Instruments (RBI/DPSS/2021-22/82)

## Chapter I: PPI Categories & KYC Limits

### Clause 1.1: Small PPIs vs Full-KYC PPIs
1. **Small PPIs (Cash Loading with Minimum Details):**
   * Maximum load limit: **₹10,000 per month** (total loading cannot exceed ₹1,20,000 in a financial year).
   * Valid for a maximum period of 24 months, after which full KYC conversion is mandatory.
   * Cash withdrawal from Small PPIs is strictly **prohibited**.
2. **Full-KYC PPIs:**
   * Maximum outstanding balance: **₹2,00,000** at any point in time.
   * Funds transfer and cash withdrawal permitted (up to ₹2,000 per transaction and ₹10,000 per month for bank-issued PPIs).

### Clause 1.2: Mandatory Interoperability via UPI
All Full-KYC prepaid payment instruments (PPIs / Mobile Wallets) must be fully **interoperable** through UPI rails and card networks.

## Chapter II: Escrow Account & Co-Branding Arrangements

### Clause 2.1: Escrow Balance Maintenance
Non-bank PPI issuers must maintain 100% of outstanding customer balances in an **Escrow Account** with a Scheduled Commercial Bank. The balance in the Escrow account shall not be co-mingled with the issuer's operational funds.

### Clause 2.2: Co-Branded PPI Restrictions
In co-branded PPI arrangements, the principal issuer remains 100% legally accountable for customer due diligence (CDD), dispute redressal, and regulatory compliance.
