# RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending

## 1. Regulatory Architecture & Scope
Applies to all digital lending operations carried out by Scheduled Commercial Banks, Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), Primary Urban Co-operative Banks, and their contracted Lending Service Providers (LSPs) operating Digital Lending Apps (DLAs).

---

## 2. Direct Fund Flow Architecture

### Section 4.1: Direct Bank-to-Borrower Account Transfers
* **Absolute Ban on Escrow / Pool Accounts:** All loan disbursements and repayments must be executed **directly between the bank account of the Regulated Entity (RE) and the bank account of the borrower**.
* Under no circumstances shall loan disbursements or borrower repayments pass through any pool, transit, or escrow account controlled by the Lending Service Provider (LSP) or third-party FinTech app.

---

## 3. Key Fact Statement (KFS) & Disclosures

### Section 6.2: Standardized Key Fact Statement (KFS)
* A standardized **Key Fact Statement (KFS)** must be provided to the borrower prior to loan agreement execution.
* The KFS must explicitly state:
  1. Annualised Percentage Rate (APR) inclusive of all processing fees, verification costs, and documentation charges.
  2. Recovery agent assignment details and grievance nodal officer contact.
  3. Look-up / Cooling-off period duration.

### Section 7.1: Look-up / Cooling-Off Period
* Borrowers must be provided a mandatory **Cooling-Off / Look-up period** (minimum **3 days** for loans with tenor $\ge$ 7 days, and **1 day** for loans with tenor < 7 days) during which they can exit the digital loan by paying the principal and proportionate APR without any prepayment penalty.

---

## 4. Borrower Data Protection & DLA Permissions

### Section 9.3: Restrictions on Mobile Device Data Access
* Digital Lending Apps (DLAs) are **strictly prohibited** from accessing mobile device data including:
  - Phone Contact Lists
  - Media / Photo Gallery / Files
  - Call Logs
* **Permitted Access (One-time):** Camera, microphone, and location data solely for KYC onboarding and V-CIP purposes with explicit user consent.
* Biometric data shall not be stored by DLAs or LSPs under any circumstances.
