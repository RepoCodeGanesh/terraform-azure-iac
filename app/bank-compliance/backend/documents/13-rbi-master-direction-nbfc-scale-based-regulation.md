# Master Direction on NBFC Scale-Based Regulation (SBR) (RBI/2022-23/29)

## Chapter I: Four-Tier Regulatory Framework for NBFCs

### Clause 1.1: Four Layers of Scale-Based Regulation (SBR)
RBI categorizes Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) into four distinct regulatory tiers based on size, activity, and systemic risk:
1. **Base Layer (NBFC-BL):** Non-deposit taking NBFCs with asset size below ₹1,000 Crore, Peer-to-Peer (P2P) lending platforms, Account Aggregators (AA), and Non-Operative Financial Holding Companies (NOFHC).
2. **Middle Layer (NBFC-ML):** All deposit-taking NBFCs (NBFC-D) regardless of asset size, and non-deposit taking NBFCs with asset size of **₹1,000 Crore and above**, Standalone Primary Dealers (SPDs), and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs).
3. **Upper Layer (NBFC-UL):** Top 10 systemically significant NBFCs specifically identified by RBI based on asset size, leverage, interconnectedness, and complexity (subject to bank-like prudential norms).
4. **Top Layer (NBFC-TL):** Reserved for NBFCs in the Upper Layer that exhibit extreme systemic risk spillovers (subject to customized higher capital and supervisory mandates).

### Clause 1.2: Net Owned Funds (NOF) Mandate
* **Minimum NOF Threshold:** The minimum Net Owned Fund (NOF) requirement for NBFC-Investment and Credit Company (NBFC-ICC), NBFC-MFI, and Factor is **₹10 Crore**.
* **P2P & AA Platforms:** Minimum NOF of **₹2 Crore**.

## Chapter II: Prudential Norms, NPA Classification & Capital Adequacy

### Clause 2.1: Harmonized 90-Day NPA Classification Norm
* **NPA Overdue Timeline:** For all layers of NBFCs (Base, Middle, Upper), overdue loans must be classified as Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) if principal or interest remains overdue for more than **90 calendar days**.
* **Upgradation Norm:** An NPA account may be upgraded to 'standard' asset status strictly after full settlement of all arrears of interest and principal by the borrower.

### Clause 2.2: Capital Adequacy (CRAR) for NBFCs
* **Minimum Capital Ratio:** All NBFCs in Base, Middle, and Upper Layers shall maintain a minimum Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) of **15%** on an ongoing basis.
* **Tier 1 Capital:** Minimum Tier 1 Capital shall not be less than **10%**.

## Chapter III: Governance, Co-Lending & Digital Lending Conduct

### Clause 3.1: Co-Lending by NBFCs with Scheduled Commercial Banks
Under the Co-Lending Model (CLM), the NBFC shall retain a minimum of **20% share** of the individual loans on its books, ensuring risk alignment with the partner bank.

### Clause 3.2: Loan Recovery Agent Standards & Fair Practices
NBFCs shall strictly ensure that their Direct Recovery Agents (DRAs) adhere to the Fair Practices Code:
(a) Recovery calls strictly permitted only between **8:00 AM and 7:00 PM**;
(b) Zero tolerance for abusive language, intimidation, harassment, or contacting friends/family;
(c) Mandatory prior disclosure of the recovery agent's identity to the borrower.
