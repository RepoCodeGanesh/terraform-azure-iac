# RBI Master Direction — Credit Card and Debit Card – Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022

## 1. Scope & Applicability
Issued under Sections 35A and 36(1)(a) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Regulates the governance, issuance, billing, and grievance redressal for Credit and Debit cards issued by all Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

---

## 2. Card Issuance Governance & Unsolicited Cards

### Section 6.1: Strict Prohibition of Unsolicited Cards & Penalties
* **Explicit Consent Required:** Card-issuers are strictly prohibited from issuing unsolicited credit cards or upgrading existing cards without the **explicit written/digital consent** of the customer.
* **Mandatory Penalty:** If an unsolicited card is issued or activated without consent and billed, the card-issuer must immediately cancel the card and pay a **penalty of double the billed amount** to the customer without waiting for an official complaint.

### Section 6.3: Mandatory OTP Consent for Card Activation
* If a newly issued credit card is not activated by the cardholder within **30 days** from the date of dispatch, the card-issuer must seek an OTP-based confirmation for activation.
* If no activation consent is received within **7 working days** of the prompt, the credit card account must be closed immediately with zero fees/charges.

---

## 3. Billing Governance & Grace Periods

### Section 9.2: Billing Cycle & Interest-Free Period
* Card-issuers must ensure that the Key Fact Statement (KFS) clearly discloses the Annualised Percentage Rate (APR) and compounding frequency.
* **Interest-Free Grace Period:** A minimum interest-free period of **not less than 15 days** must be provided between the bill generation date and the payment due date.
* **Payment Processing Timelines:** Any payment made by the cardholder must be credited to the card account on the transaction date itself, and late fees shall not be levied if payment is cleared within 3 days of the due date (Grace Day Rule).

---

## 4. Customer Liability in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions

### Section 12: Zero & Limited Liability Tiers

| Incident Scenario | Customer Reporting Window | Customer Financial Liability |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Contributory Fraud / System Negligence by Bank** | Irrespective of reporting time | **Zero Liability (100% Bank Reversal)** |
| **Third-Party Breach (Neither Bank nor Customer Negligence)** | Reported within **3 Working Days** | **Zero Liability** |
| **Third-Party Breach** | Reported within **4 to 7 Working Days** | Capped at Transaction Value or **₹10,000** (whichever is lower) |
| **Third-Party Breach** | Reported beyond **7 Working Days** | As per the approved Board Policy of the Bank |
