# RBI Master Direction — Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016

## 1. Overview & Statutory Authority
Issued under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, and Rule 9(14) of the Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005. Applies to all Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs), Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

---

## 2. Customer Identification & Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)

### Section 4.1: Standard Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)
For individual residents, proof of identity and address must be established using one of the following:
1. **Passport**
2. **Driving Licence**
3. **Proof of possession of Aadhaar number** (offline verification / Aadhaar XML / masked Aadhaar with only last 4 digits visible)
4. **Voter's Identity Card** issued by Election Commission of India
5. **Job card issued by NREGA** signed by a State Government officer
6. **Letter issued by National Population Register (NPR)** containing details of name and address.

> **Mandatory Rule:** PAN or Form 60 is mandatory for all account-opening operations per Rule 114B of the Income Tax Rules.

---

### Section 4.2(a): Simplified KYC for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs)
For Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs):
1. **Acceptable Overseas Documents:**
   - Overseas Passport with valid Indian Visa / OCI card.
   - Foreign address proof attested by Indian Embassy / High Commission / Notary Public / Overseas branch of Scheduled Commercial Bank.
   - Overseas utility bills (not older than 2 months) or valid employment permit.
2. **Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP):**
   - V-CIP is permitted for NRI account opening provided the geolocation check confirms overseas residency during the live session.
   - Must incorporate live liveness detection, facial matching against official passport photo, and OTP verification via registered mobile/email.

---

## 3. Video-Based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP)

### Section 18.1: Operational Controls for V-CIP
1. **Live Geolocation:** Application must ensure the customer is physically present within the allowed territory/jurisdiction via live GPS tagging.
2. **Liveness Detection:** AI-assisted anti-spoofing and liveness detection must be performed in real time.
3. **Authorized Bank Officer:** V-CIP must be conducted by trained bank personnel directly. Third-party vendor agents cannot conduct the final customer verification.
4. **Encrypted Video Archives:** Video recording, audio feed, and metadata must be stored in tamper-proof encrypted storage within India for a minimum period of **5 years** post account closure.

---

## 4. Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) & Simplified Due Diligence

### Section 23: CKYC Number Integration
* Regulated Entities (REs) must upload the KYC data to the Central KYC Records Registry (CKYCR) within **10 days** of commencing an account-based relationship.
* Once a 14-digit CKYC Identifier is assigned, the customer shall not be asked for fresh KYC documents by any other regulated financial institution unless there is a change of address or identity details.

---

## 5. Periodic Updation of KYC (Re-KYC)

| Risk Classification | Mandatory Updation Frequency | Process Requirements |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **High Risk** | Every **2 Years** | Fresh OVD submission, physical or digital address verification, and screening against sanction lists (UNSCR, FATF). |
| **Medium Risk** | Every **8 Years** | Self-declaration if no change in address/status; OVD submission if details changed. |
| **Low Risk** | Every **10 Years** | Digital self-declaration through Internet Banking / Mobile App / ATM / Email if no changes. |

---

## 6. Beneficial Ownership & Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)

### Section 33: Beneficial Ownership Thresholds
* For Companies: Natural person(s) controlling more than **10%** of shares/capital/profits.
* For Partnership Firms / Trust: Natural person(s) holding more than **10%** of property/capital/profits.
* For Unincorporated Associations: Natural person holding more than **15%** of property or interest.

### Section 41: Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
* Opening accounts for PEPs requires **Senior Management Approval**.
* Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is mandatory, including verifying the source of funds and continuous transaction monitoring.

---

## 7. Penalty & Non-Compliance
Failure to comply with KYC / AML guidelines constitutes a violation under Section 47A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, subjecting the regulated entity to monetary penalties, operational restrictions on onboarding new customers, and regulatory supervisory sanctions.
