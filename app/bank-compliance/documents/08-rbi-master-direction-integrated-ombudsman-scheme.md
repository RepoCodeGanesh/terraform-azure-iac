# Reserve Bank — Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RBI/2021-22/126)

## Chapter I: Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Resolution Timelines

### Clause 1.1: Internal Ombudsman (IO) Mandate
All Scheduled Commercial Banks with 10 or more banking outlets must appoint an Internal Ombudsman (IO). Any customer complaint rejected wholly or partly by the bank must be automatically examined by the Internal Ombudsman before formal communication of rejection to the customer.

### Clause 1.2: Thirty (30) Day Resolution SLA
* **Internal Resolution:** The Regulated Entity (Bank) must resolve customer complaints within a maximum period of **thirty (30) calendar days** from the date of receipt.
* **Escalation to RBI Ombudsman:** If the bank fails to provide a reply within 30 days or if the complainant is dissatisfied with the response, the customer has the statutory right to file an appeal before the RBI Ombudsman (CMS Portal).

## Chapter II: Compensation Framework & Limited Customer Liability

### Clause 2.1: Zero Liability in Unauthorized Electronic Transactions
A customer shall have **Zero Liability** in the following events:
(a) Contributory fraud/negligence/deficiency on the part of the bank;
(b) Third-party breach where the deficiency lies neither with the bank nor the customer, provided the customer notifies the bank within **three (3) working days** of receiving the transaction alert.

### Clause 2.2: Turnaround Time (TAT) for Failed Transactions (Harmonized TAT)
* **ATM / UPI / IMPS Failed Debits:** Must be reversed within **T + 1 day**.
* **Compensation for Delay:** If reversal is delayed beyond T + 1, the bank shall pay statutory compensation of **₹100 per day** of delay directly to the customer's account without requiring a separate claim.
