# Master Direction on Frauds — Classification and Reporting (RBI/DBS/2016-17/28)

## Chapter I: Classification of Frauds & Central Fraud Registry (CFR)

### Clause 1.1: Mandatory Classification Categories
Banks shall classify frauds into the following categories:
(a) Misappropriation and criminal breach of trust;
(b) Fraudulent encashment through forged instruments or manipulation of books;
(c) Unauthorized credit facilities extended for reward or illegal gratification;
(d) Negligence and cash shortages;
(e) Cheating and forgery in loan sanctioning;
(f) Fraudulent electronic transactions and cyber frauds.

### Clause 1.2: Central Fraud Registry (CFR) Reporting SLA
* **Reporting Timeline:** All fraud cases involving ₹1 Lakh and above must be reported on the web-based Central Fraud Registry (CFR) within **three (3) weeks** of detection (FMR-1 format).
* **High-Value Fraud Flash Reports:** For frauds involving ₹5 Crore and above, banks must submit a preliminary flash report to RBI Department of Banking Supervision within **48 hours** of detection.

## Chapter II: Early Warning Signals (EWS) & Red Flagged Accounts (RFA)

### Clause 2.1: Early Warning Signals (EWS) Framework
Banks must implement an automated Early Warning Signals (EWS) system tracking 42 mandatory triggers, including:
(a) Default in payment to statutory bodies;
(b) Frequent bouncing of high-value cheques;
(c) Significant divergence in financial statements submitted to banks vs MCA;
(d) Routing of funds through non-consortium accounts.

### Clause 2.2: Red Flagged Account (RFA) Threshold & Forensic Audit
* **RFA Threshold:** Accounts with exposure of ₹50 Crore or more meeting EWS triggers must be red-flagged immediately.
* **Forensic Audit Mandate:** A forensic audit must be initiated within 30 days of red-flagging, and completed within 90 days.
* **Consortium Decision:** If an account is classified as fraud by one bank in a consortium, all member banks must mirror the classification within 15 days.
