# Master Direction on Basel III Capital Regulations (RBI/2015-16/58)

## Chapter I: Minimum Capital Adequacy & Capital Conservation Buffer

### Clause 1.1: Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) & CRAR Thresholds
Commercial banks in India shall maintain a minimum regulatory capital structure on an ongoing basis:
* **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1):** Minimum **5.50%** of total Risk Weighted Assets (RWAs).
* **Tier 1 Capital:** Minimum **7.00%** of total RWAs.
* **Total Capital (CRAR):** Minimum **9.00%** of total RWAs (excluding buffer).
* **Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB):** An additional **2.50%** comprised strictly of Common Equity Tier 1 capital, bringing overall minimum CRAR to **11.50%**.

### Clause 1.2: Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs) Surcharge
Banks identified as D-SIBs (SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank) must maintain an additional CET1 capital surcharge ranging from **0.20% to 0.80%** over and above the baseline capital conservation buffer.

## Chapter II: Liquidity Risk & Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)

### Clause 2.1: Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) 100% Mandate
Banks must maintain an adequate stock of unencumbered High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) that can be converted into cash easily and immediately to meet liquidity needs for a 30-calendar-day liquidity stress scenario.
* **LCR Formula:** $\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{Stock of High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)}}{\text{Total Net Cash Outflows over 30 days}} \ge 100\%$
* **Reporting:** Banks must compute and disclose daily LCR and maintain 100% compliance at all times.

### Clause 2.2: Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)
Banks must maintain a minimum Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) of **100%** on an ongoing basis to ensure resilience over a one-year horizon.
