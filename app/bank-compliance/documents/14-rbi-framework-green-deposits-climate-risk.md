# Framework for Acceptance of Green Deposits & Climate Risk Disclosures (RBI/2023-24/14)

## Chapter I: Green Deposits Issuance & Allocation Framework

### Clause 1.1: Permissible Green Activities
Regulated Entities (REs) shall raise Green Deposits exclusively denominated in Indian Rupees (INR) to finance eligible green projects, including:
(a) Renewable energy (solar, wind, biomass, hydro);
(b) Energy efficiency and green building infrastructure;
(c) Clean transportation (electric vehicles, charging infrastructure);
(d) Climate change adaptation and sustainable water management.

### Clause 1.2: Excluded Sectors & Prohibited Allocations
Green deposit proceeds shall strictly **NOT** be allocated to:
(a) Projects involving fossil fuel extraction or refining;
(b) Nuclear power generation;
(c) Weapons, tobacco, or gambling enterprises;
(d) Hydropower projects with generation capacity exceeding 25 MW.

## Chapter II: Third-Party Verification & Climate Stress Testing

### Clause 2.1: Independent Third-Party Assurance
Banks issuing green deposits must obtain independent third-party impact assessment and external verification on an annual basis regarding the allocation of proceeds.

### Clause 2.2: Climate Risk & Scenario Stress Testing
Banks shall integrate climate-related financial risks (physical and transition risks) into their Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP) and conduct regular climate scenario stress testing.
