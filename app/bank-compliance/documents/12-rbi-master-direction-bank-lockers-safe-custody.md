# Master Direction on Safe Deposit Lockers (RBI/2021-22/86)

## Chapter I: Locker Agreement & Security Infrastructure

### Clause 1.1: Model Locker Agreement
Banks shall adopt the IBA model locker agreement conforming to Supreme Court of India directives. Banks shall not incorporate unfair or one-sided liability waiver clauses in the locker agreement.

### Clause 1.2: Electronic Access & CCTV Recording Mandate
* **CCTV Footage Retention:** Banks must record and preserve CCTV camera footage of the locker vault entry and exit gates for a minimum period of **180 days** (six months).
* **Instant SMS / Email Alerts:** Banks must send an immediate automated SMS and email alert to the registered customer whenever a locker is accessed.

## Chapter II: Bank Liability & Compensation Norms

### Clause 1.1: Bank Liability for Loss of Contents (100x Rule)
In case of theft, burglary, dacoity, robbery, building collapse, or fire in the bank's premises due to the bank's negligence or security failure, the bank's compensation liability to the locker hirer shall be equivalent to **one hundred (100) times the prevailing annual rent** of the safe deposit locker.

### Clause 2.2: Unclaimed & Inoperative Lockers Break-Open Protocol
If a locker remains inoperative for seven (7) years and the hirer cannot be traced despite registered notices, the bank may break open the locker in the presence of two independent witnesses and a bank officer, with the entire break-open process video-recorded.
