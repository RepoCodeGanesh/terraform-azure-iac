HOUSING TAX BENEFITS: HRA EXEMPTION & HOME LOAN TAX DEDUCTIONS
================================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 10(13A), Rule 2A, Section 24(b) | FY 2026-27

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE (HRA) EXEMPTION — SECTION 10(13A)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Applicability: Salaried employees receiving HRA who live in rented accommodation (Old Regime Only).

HRA EXEMPTION COMPUTATION FORMULA:
The exempt HRA amount is the MINIMUM of the following three:
  1. Actual HRA received from employer
  2. Rent paid minus 10% of Basic Salary (+ DA)
  3. 50% of Basic Salary (if living in Metro: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) OR
     40% of Basic Salary (if living in Non-Metro)

DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS:
  • Rent receipts for the financial year.
  • Landlord PAN mandatory if annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000.
  • Rental agreement.

2. HOME LOAN INTEREST & PRINCIPAL DEDUCTION — SECTION 24(b) & 80C
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
INTEREST DEDUCTION (SECTION 24b):
  • Self-Occupied Property: Interest deduction up to ₹2,00,000 per financial year (Old Regime).
  • Let-Out Property: Full interest paid is deductible against rental income. Loss from house property capped at ₹2,00,000 setoff against other income.
  • Construction condition: Construction must be completed within 5 years of loan sanction.

PRINCIPAL DEDUCTION (SECTION 80C):
  • Principal repayment deductible up to ₹1,50,000 under Section 80C.
  • Stamp duty and registration charges are also deductible under Section 80C in the year of purchase.

3. CAN YOU CLAIM BOTH HRA AND HOME LOAN INTEREST?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
YES, under the following scenarios:
  1. Own home is in a different city due to employment/business in another city.
  2. Own home is under construction or let out, and you reside in rented premises.
  3. Own home is in the same city but far from workplace (justified under genuine commercial reasons).
  Result: Claim HRA exemption under 10(13A) AND Home Loan interest under 24(b) simultaneously!
