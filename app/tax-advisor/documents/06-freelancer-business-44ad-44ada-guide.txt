FREELANCERS, PROFESSIONALS & SMALL BUSINESS TAXATION (44AD & 44ADA)
====================================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 44AD, Section 44ADA | FY 2026-27

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. SECTION 44ADA — PRESUMPTIVE TAXATION FOR PROFESSIONALS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ELIGIBLE PROFESSIONALS:
  Software Developers, IT Consultants, Doctors, Lawyers, Engineers, Architects, CAs, Interior Decorators, Film Artists, Consultants, Freelancers.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
  • Gross professional receipts up to ₹75,000,000 (₹75 Lakh) per financial year (if digital receipts >= 95%).
  • Standard limit: ₹50 Lakh if cash receipts > 5%.

HOW IT WORKS:
  • 50% of Total Gross Receipts is presumed as Net Taxable Income.
  • Remaining 50% is treated as business expenses without keeping any books of accounts, bills, or receipts!
  • Example: Freelance software developer earning ₹40 Lakhs/yr.
    Presumed Taxable Income = ₹20 Lakhs (50% of ₹40L).
    Tax is calculated on ₹20 Lakhs under New or Old Regime!
  • No Tax Audit under Section 44AB required if declaring >= 50% profits.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SECTION 44AD — PRESUMPTIVE TAXATION FOR SMALL BUSINESSES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ELIGIBLE BUSINESSES:
  Retail traders, wholesalers, manufacturers, service providers (except professionals, agency business, or commission agents).

TURNOVER CEILING:
  • Up to ₹3,00,000,000 (₹3 Crore) if digital receipts >= 95%.
  • Standard limit: ₹2 Crore.

PRESUMED PROFIT RATE:
  • 6% of total turnover received through digital/bank channels (UPI, NEFT, RTGS, Cards).
  • 8% of total turnover received in cash.

3. ADVANCE TAX RESPONSIBILITY FOR PRESUMPTIVE TAXPAYERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Taxpayers opting for Section 44AD / 44ADA must pay 100% of their estimated advance tax in a SINGLE installment on or before 15 MARCH of the financial year.
