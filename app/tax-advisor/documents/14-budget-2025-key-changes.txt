BUDGET 2025 KEY TAX CHANGES — FY 2025-26
=========================================

SOURCE: Finance Act 2025 (Union Budget 2025-26), CBDT Circulars

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. ZERO TAX UP TO ₹12 LAKH — BIGGEST CHANGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Section 87A rebate enhanced to ₹60,000 under new tax regime.
Net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 → ZERO tax.
For salaried with ₹75K standard deduction:
  Gross salary up to ₹12,75,000 → ZERO tax.
This is a major relief for middle-income earners (previously ₹7L limit).

NEW REGIME REVISED SLABS (FY 2025-26):
  Up to ₹4L:     0%
  ₹4L–₹8L:       5%
  ₹8L–₹12L:      10%
  ₹12L–₹16L:     15%
  ₹16L–₹20L:     20%
  ₹20L–₹24L:     25%
  Above ₹24L:    30%
(Previous structure had ₹3L zero, different brackets)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. EMPLOYER NPS LIMIT INCREASED TO 14% (Budget 2024 effect)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Section 80CCD(2): Employer NPS contribution limit raised from 10% to 14% for private sector.
Previously 14% only for central government employees.
Now uniformly 14% for ALL employees.
Works in BOTH old and new tax regime.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. STANDARD DEDUCTION INCREASED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
New regime: ₹75,000 (increased from ₹50,000 in Budget 2024)
Old regime: ₹50,000 (unchanged)
Family pension: ₹25,000 (new regime, Budget 2024)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. LTCG ON EQUITY — 12.5% (Budget 2024)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LTCG on listed equity / equity MF increased from 10% to 12.5%.
STCG increased from 15% to 20%.
LTCG exemption increased from ₹1L to ₹1.25L per year.
No indexation benefit retained for equity.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. REAL ESTATE — INDEXATION REMOVED (Budget 2024)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For property sold after 23 July 2024:
  LTCG rate: 12.5% without indexation
  Exception (grandfathering): For property bought before 23 July 2024:
    Choose LOWER of: 12.5% without indexation vs 20% with indexation

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
6. UPDATED RETURN — 4 YEARS NOW ALLOWED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Updated return (Section 139(8A)) can now be filed up to 4 years from end of assessment year.
Earlier: 2 years only.
Additional tax: 25% if filed within 12 months, 50% if within 24 months, 60% if 25-48 months.
Use this to correct mistakes or disclose missed income.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
7. TDS RATE RATIONALISATION (Budget 2024)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Several TDS rates reduced:
  - Section 194D (insurance commission): 5% → 2%
  - Section 194DA (life insurance maturity): 5% → 2%
  - Section 194G (lottery commission): 5% → 2%
  - Section 194H (commission/brokerage): 5% → 2%
  - Section 194IB (rent by individual): 5% → 2%
  - Section 194M (professional fees by individual): 5% → 2%
TDS threshold for rent (Section 194I): No change.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
8. NPS VATSALYA SCHEME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
New NPS scheme for minors, opened by parents.
Contribution by parent qualifies for Section 80CCD(1B) (within ₹50K limit)
At 18, minor's NPS Vatsalya converts to regular NPS Tier-1.
Partial withdrawal allowed after 3 years for education/disability.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
9. PRESUMPTIVE TAXATION THRESHOLD ENHANCED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Section 44ADA (professionals): Threshold raised to ₹75 lakh if ≥50% of receipts are digital.
Section 44AD (small business): ₹3 crore if ≥50% receipts digital.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
10. VIVAD SE VISHWAS SCHEME 2024
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Dispute resolution scheme for pending income tax litigation.
Pay reduced tax and waiver of interest/penalty.
Applicable for cases pending before CIT(Appeals), ITAT, High Court, Supreme Court.
