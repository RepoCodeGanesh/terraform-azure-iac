BUDGET 2025 KEY TAX CHANGES — FY 2026-27
=========================================

SOURCE: Finance Act 2025 (Union Budget 2025-26), CBDT Circulars

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. ZERO TAX UP TO ₹12 LAKH — BIGGEST CHANGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Section 87A rebate enhanced to ₹60,000 under new tax regime.
Net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 → ZERO tax.
For salaried with ₹75K standard deduction:
  Gross salary up to ₹12,75,000 → ZERO tax.
This is a major relief for middle-income earners (previously ₹7L limit).

NEW REGIME REVISED SLABS (FY 2026-27):
  Up to ₹4L:     0%
  ₹4L–₹8L:       5%
  ₹8L–₹12L:      10%
  ₹12L–₹16L:     15%
  ₹16L–₹20L:     20%
  ₹20L–₹24L:     25%
  Above ₹24L:    30%

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. EMPLOYER NPS LIMIT INCREASED TO 14%
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Section 80CCD(2): Employer NPS contribution limit raised from 10% to 14% for private sector.
Now uniformly 14% for ALL employees.
Works in BOTH old and new tax regime.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. STANDARD DEDUCTION INCREASED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
New regime: ₹75,000
Old regime: ₹50,000
Family pension: ₹25,000 (new regime)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. LTCG ON EQUITY — 12.5%
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LTCG on listed equity / equity MF increased to 12.5%.
STCG increased to 20%.
LTCG exemption increased to ₹1.25L per year.
