SECTION 80D — DEDUCTION FOR HEALTH INSURANCE PREMIUM
======================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 80D
AVAILABILITY: OLD TAX REGIME ONLY

DEDUCTION LIMITS:

1. FOR SELF, SPOUSE AND DEPENDENT CHILDREN:
   - Health insurance premium: up to ₹25,000
   - Preventive health check-up (within this limit): up to ₹5,000

2. FOR PARENTS (additional, over and above above):
   - Parents below 60 years: up to ₹25,000
   - Parents aged 60 years or above (Senior Citizen): up to ₹50,000

3. MAXIMUM COMBINED DEDUCTION:
   - Self + family (non-senior) + parents (non-senior): ₹25,000 + ₹25,000 = ₹50,000
   - Self + family (non-senior) + parents (senior citizen): ₹25,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹75,000
   - Self + family (senior citizen) + parents (senior citizen): ₹50,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹1,00,000
   - Taxpayer is senior citizen, no parents: ₹50,000

4. PREVENTIVE HEALTH CHECK-UP (Sub-limit):
   - ₹5,000 can be claimed for preventive health check-up
   - Cash payment is ALLOWED for preventive health check-up (unlike premium)
   - Included within the overall 80D limit (not additional)

5. PAYMENT MODE:
   - Premium must be paid by any mode OTHER than cash
   - Credit card, debit card, net banking, cheque — all valid
   - EXCEPTION: Preventive health check-up can be paid in cash

6. ELIGIBLE POLICIES:
   - Health insurance from insurance companies
   - Mediclaim policies
   - Critical illness riders (if separately billed)
   - Contribution to Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)
   - Group health insurance (employer-provided) — premium paid by employee qualifies

7. WHAT DOES NOT QUALIFY:
   - Premium paid for siblings, in-laws (unless dependent)
   - Group health insurance premium paid entirely by employer
   - Life insurance premium (covered under 80C)
   - Premium paid in cash (except preventive health check-up)

8. SENIOR CITIZEN WITHOUT HEALTH INSURANCE:
   - If senior citizen taxpayer pays medical expenses themselves (no insurance available)
   - Deduction of ₹50,000 allowed for actual medical expenditure
   - This provision is for senior citizens who are unable to get health insurance

PRACTICAL EXAMPLES:

Example 1: Age 35, parents age 65
  Self + family premium: ₹15,000
  Parents premium: ₹35,000
  Total 80D deduction: ₹15,000 + ₹35,000 = ₹50,000 (parents are senior citizens, so ₹50K limit)
  Tax saving at 30% slab: ₹15,000

Example 2: Age 62 (senior citizen), parents not alive
  Own premium: ₹45,000
  Deduction: ₹45,000 (capped at ₹50,000 for senior citizen)

Example 3: Age 30, parents age 55
  Self premium: ₹20,000
  Parents premium: ₹20,000
  Preventive check-up: ₹3,000 (for self — counted within ₹25K limit)
  Total deduction: ₹20,000 + ₹20,000 = ₹40,000

TIPS:
- Buy separate family floater policy for self+spouse+children
- Buy separate individual policy for parents (or senior citizen floater)
- Preventive health check-up receipts should be preserved
- Policy must be in force during the financial year claimed
