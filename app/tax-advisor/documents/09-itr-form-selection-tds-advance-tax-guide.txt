1. WHICH ITR FORM TO FILE?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ITR-1 (SAHAJ):
  ✅ Resident Individuals | Income up to ₹50 Lakh | Salary/Pension + One House Property + Other Sources (Interest/Dividend).
  ❌ Disqualified if: Capital Gains exist, >1 House Property, Business Income, Unlisted Shares, Foreign Assets/Income, Director in company.

ITR-2:
  ✅ Salaried with Capital Gains (LTCG/STCG), Multiple Properties, Income > ₹50L, Foreign Assets, Foreign Income, NRIs, ESOPs, Directors.

ITR-3:
  ✅ Business / Professional Income (Not opting 44ADA), F&O Trading, Intraday Stock Trading, Partnership Firm Income + Capital Gains.

ITR-4 (SUGAM):
  ✅ Small Businesses opting Section 44AD or Professionals opting Section 44ADA (Presumptive Income).

2. ADVANCE TAX DUE DATES & INTEREST (SECTION 207-219)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Required if net annual tax liability (after TDS) exceeds ₹10,000.
  • 15 June:      15% of estimated tax
  • 15 September: 45% of estimated tax
  • 15 December:  75% of estimated tax
  • 15 March:     100% of estimated tax
  Interest under 234B & 234C: 1% per month for delay/deferment.
