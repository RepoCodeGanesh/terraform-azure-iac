CAPITAL GAINS TAX — FY 2026-27
================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 112, 112A, 111A as amended by Finance Act 2024 / 2025

EQUITY AND EQUITY MUTUAL FUNDS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LONG TERM CAPITAL GAINS (LTCG) — Section 112A
  Holding period: More than 12 months
  Tax rate: 12.5% (FLAT, no indexation)
  Exemption: First ₹1,25,000 of LTCG is exempt (enhanced from ₹1L)
  Applies to: Listed equity shares, equity-oriented mutual funds, units of business trust
  STT paid: YES (required)
  87A rebate: NOT available on LTCG under 112A
  
  Example:
    LTCG from equity MF: ₹3,00,000
    Exemption: (₹1,25,000)
    Taxable LTCG: ₹1,75,000
    Tax @ 12.5%: ₹21,875 + cess
  
  Note: Even if your total income is below ₹5L (old) or ₹12L (new),
  LTCG on equity still gets taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25L.

SHORT TERM CAPITAL GAINS (STCG) — Section 111A
  Holding period: 12 months or less
  Tax rate: 20% FLAT
  Applies to: Listed equity shares, equity-oriented mutual funds
  STT paid: YES (required)
  
  Example:
    STCG from equity SIP redemption: ₹50,000
    Tax @ 20%: ₹10,000 + cess

DEBT MUTUAL FUNDS AND OTHER ASSETS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DEBT MUTUAL FUNDS (post March 2023):
  - ALL gains taxed at SLAB RATE regardless of holding period
  - No LTCG/STCG distinction for debt funds purchased after April 2023
  - Indexation: NOT available for debt funds post March 2023

GOLD ETF / GOLD MUTUAL FUNDS:
  Holding period for LTCG: More than 24 months
  LTCG rate: 12.5% without indexation
  STCG: Slab rate

REAL ESTATE / PROPERTY:
  Holding period for LTCG: More than 24 months
  LTCG rate: 12.5% WITHOUT indexation
    Exception: Option available for property purchased before 23 July 2024:
    12.5% without indexation OR 20% with indexation (whichever is lower)
  STCG: Slab rate
  
  Section 54: Exempt LTCG on residential property if reinvested in another residential property
  Section 54EC: Invest LTCG in NHAI/REC bonds within 6 months (up to ₹50L, 5-year lock-in)

CRYPTO AND VIRTUAL DIGITAL ASSETS (VDA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Tax rate: 30% FLAT on ALL gains (no holding period distinction)
  No deduction other than cost of acquisition
  1% TDS deducted at source on transfer
  Cannot offset crypto losses against other income or crypto gains

ITR FORM FOR CAPITAL GAINS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  LTCG/STCG income → Must file ITR-2 (salaried with capital gains)
  ITR-1 (Sahaj) CANNOT be used if you have any capital gains
  
  For LTCG up to ₹1.25L (all exempt): Still report in ITR-2, no tax
