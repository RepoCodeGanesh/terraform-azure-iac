SECTION 80C — DEDUCTIONS FROM GROSS TOTAL INCOME
==================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 80C | Limit: ₹1,50,000 per year

IMPORTANT: Section 80C deductions are available ONLY under the OLD tax regime.
They are NOT available under the New Tax Regime (except employer NPS via 80CCD(2)).

MAXIMUM DEDUCTION UNDER 80C: ₹1,50,000 per financial year
(Combined limit for all 80C investments together)

ELIGIBLE INVESTMENTS AND PAYMENTS:

1. EMPLOYEE PROVIDENT FUND (EPF)
   - Employee's own contribution to EPF is eligible
   - Employer's contribution is NOT counted under 80C
   - EPF interest is tax-free (up to threshold)
   - Maturity amount is tax-free after 5 years of continuous service

2. PUBLIC PROVIDENT FUND (PPF)
   - Annual investment: min ₹500, max ₹1,50,000
   - Lock-in: 15 years (partial withdrawal after 7 years)
   - Interest rate: 7.1% p.a. (Q1 FY26, government-declared quarterly)
   - Interest and maturity: fully tax-free (EEE status)
   - Ideal for long-term, risk-free tax saving

3. EQUITY LINKED SAVINGS SCHEME (ELSS)
   - Mutual funds with 3-year lock-in (shortest among 80C options)
   - Market-linked returns (historically 12-15% p.a.)
   - LTCG on gains above ₹1L taxed at 12.5%
   - Best for wealth creation + tax saving
   - Can start SIP as low as ₹500/month

4. LIFE INSURANCE PREMIUM (LIC/Term/ULIP)
   - Premium paid for self, spouse, and children eligible
   - Policy should be in taxpayer's name or spouse/child
   - For policies issued after 1 April 2012: premium must not exceed 10% of sum assured
   - Term insurance premiums qualify
   - ULIP maturity: tax-free if annual premium ≤ ₹2.5L

5. NATIONAL SAVINGS CERTIFICATE (NSC)
   - 5-year lock-in
   - Interest rate: 7.7% p.a. (compounded annually, Q1 FY26)
   - Interest accrued annually is also eligible for 80C deduction (reinvestment)
   - Maturity taxable as per slab

6. TAX SAVER FIXED DEPOSIT (5-year FD)
   - Offered by banks and post offices
   - 5-year lock-in, premature withdrawal not allowed
   - Interest taxable as per slab (TDS deducted)
   - Less tax-efficient but safe

7. SUKANYA SAMRIDDHI YOJANA (SSY)
   - For girl child below 10 years
   - Interest rate: 8.2% p.a. (Q1 FY26)
   - EEE status — investment, interest, and maturity all tax-free
   - Account matures when girl turns 21

8. SENIOR CITIZEN SAVINGS SCHEME (SCSS)
   - For individuals 60+ years
   - Interest rate: 8.2% p.a. (Q1 FY26)
   - Max investment: ₹30 lakh per individual
   - 5-year tenure, extendable by 3 years
   - TDS deducted if interest > ₹50,000/year

9. HOME LOAN PRINCIPAL REPAYMENT (Section 80C)
   - Principal portion of home loan EMI qualifies
   - Property must not be sold within 5 years of possession
   - Only for residential property
   - Interest portion is covered separately under Section 24b

10. CHILDREN'S TUITION FEES
    - Full-time education at any school/college/university in India
    - For up to 2 children
    - Applies to tuition fee only (not development fee, transport, etc.)
    - Must be Indian institution

11. NATIONAL PENSION SCHEME — Employee Contribution (80CCD(1))
    - Employee's contribution to NPS Tier 1 up to 10% of salary (basic + DA)
    - This is within the overall ₹1.5L 80C limit
    - Additional ₹50,000 available under 80CCD(1B) over and above 80C limit

12. POST OFFICE TIME DEPOSIT (5-year)
    - Interest rate: 7.5% p.a. (Q1 FY26)
    - Only 5-year POTD qualifies (not 1/2/3-year)

STRATEGY TIPS:
- EPF contribution is automatic — check your annual EPF statement
- Use remaining 80C limit for PPF (safe) or ELSS (high return)
- Don't invest just for tax saving — check if investment aligns with goals
- 80C limit has not been increased since 2014; inflation reduces real value
