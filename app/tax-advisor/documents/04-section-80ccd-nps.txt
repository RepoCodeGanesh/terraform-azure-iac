NATIONAL PENSION SCHEME (NPS) — TAX BENEFITS
=============================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 80CCD(1), 80CCD(1B), 80CCD(2)

NPS OVERVIEW:
NPS is a government-sponsored retirement savings scheme regulated by PFRDA.
It has Tier 1 (mandatory, tax-advantaged) and Tier 2 (voluntary, no tax benefit).
Tax benefits apply ONLY to NPS Tier 1 contributions.

SECTION 80CCD(1) — EMPLOYEE/SELF CONTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Salaried: Up to 10% of salary (Basic + DA)
- Self-employed: Up to 20% of gross income
- This is WITHIN the overall Section 80C limit of ₹1,50,000
- AVAILABLE UNDER: OLD REGIME ONLY
- Example: Basic ₹60,000/month → annual basic ₹7,20,000 → max 80CCD(1) = ₹72,000
  (counted within 80C ₹1.5L limit)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80CCD(1B) — ADDITIONAL SELF CONTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Additional ₹50,000 deduction OVER AND ABOVE the ₹1.5L 80C limit
- Total effective 80C+80CCD(1B) limit = ₹2,00,000
- Must be voluntary contribution to NPS Tier 1
- AVAILABLE UNDER: OLD REGIME ONLY
- This is the most powerful individual tax-saving option after 80C is exhausted

Example:
  80C limit used:          ₹1,50,000
  80CCD(1B) additional:      ₹50,000
  Total deduction:          ₹2,00,000
  Tax saved at 30% slab:    ₹60,000 + 4% cess = ₹62,400

SECTION 80CCD(2) — EMPLOYER CONTRIBUTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
THIS IS THE MOST POWERFUL NPS BENEFIT — AVAILABLE IN BOTH REGIMES!

- Employer's contribution to employee's NPS account
- Limit: Up to 14% of salary (Basic + DA) for Central Government employees
         Up to 14% of salary for private sector employees (Budget 2024 increase from 10%)
- NOT included in 80C limit — completely separate
- NOT included in employee's taxable salary
- AVAILABLE UNDER: BOTH OLD AND NEW TAX REGIME ✅

Why 80CCD(2) is powerful:
  CTC ₹20L, Basic ₹10L/year
  Employer NPS @ 14%: ₹1,40,000/year
  This ₹1,40,000 is:
    - Deducted from employee's taxable salary
    - NOT counted in 80C
    - Available even in New Regime
  Tax saving at 30%: ₹42,000/year
  Net cost to employer: same CTC restructuring, employee pays less tax

HOW TO USE 80CCD(2):
  Ask HR to restructure CTC:
  - Reduce "Special Allowance" (fully taxable)
  - Add "Employer NPS Contribution" (up to 14% of basic)
  - Net take-home may slightly reduce but tax saving is significant

NPS WITHDRAWAL RULES:
  - At retirement (60 years): 60% lump sum tax-free, 40% mandatory annuity (taxable)
  - Partial withdrawal (after 3 years): up to 25% for specific reasons (home, education, illness)
  - Exit before 60: 20% lump sum, 80% mandatory annuity

TAX ON NPS MATURITY:
  - 60% lump sum at retirement: Tax-FREE (EEE for that portion)
  - Annuity income: Taxable as per slab rate

STRATEGY:
  Priority order for NPS tax benefits:
  1. Employer NPS (80CCD(2)) — ask HR to restructure, works in both regimes
  2. 80CCD(1B) extra ₹50K — only if in old regime and 80C is exhausted
  3. 80CCD(1) within 80C — use NPS Tier 1 as part of 80C allocation
