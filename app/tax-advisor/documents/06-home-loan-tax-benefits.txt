HOME LOAN TAX BENEFITS — COMPLETE GUIDE
========================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 24(b), 80C, 80EEA
AVAILABILITY: OLD TAX REGIME (Section 24b home loan interest NOT available in new regime)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. SECTION 24(b) — INTEREST ON HOME LOAN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: Up to ₹2,00,000 per year on interest paid on home loan
Regime: OLD REGIME ONLY
Conditions:
  - Property must be self-occupied (or deemed self-occupied — up to 2 properties)
  - Loan must be taken for purchase, construction, repair, or renovation
  - Construction must be completed within 5 years from end of FY in which loan was taken
    (else limit reduces to ₹30,000)
  - Property must be completed — under-construction property: no benefit until possession
    (Interest during construction period can be claimed in 5 equal installments after possession)

For let-out property:
  - No limit on interest deduction (can claim full interest paid)
  - But overall loss from house property capped at ₹2,00,000 set-off against other income
  - Remaining loss carried forward for 8 years

PRE-CONSTRUCTION INTEREST:
  - Interest paid from loan disbursement to 31st March before possession year
  - Can be claimed in 5 equal installments starting from possession year
  - Included within the ₹2L limit

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SECTION 80C — PRINCIPAL REPAYMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: Principal repayment up to ₹1,50,000 (within overall 80C limit)
Regime: OLD REGIME ONLY
Conditions:
  - For residential property only
  - Property must not be sold within 5 years of possession
    (If sold before 5 years: ALL principal deductions claimed are reversed and added to income)
  - Stamp duty and registration charges also qualify under 80C (one-time, in year of payment)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. SECTION 80EEA — ADDITIONAL INTEREST FOR FIRST-TIME BUYERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: Additional ₹1,50,000 on interest (over and above Section 24b ₹2L limit)
Total interest benefit: ₹2,00,000 + ₹1,50,000 = ₹3,50,000
Conditions:
  - First-time homebuyer (no residential property as of loan sanction date)
  - Loan sanctioned between 1 April 2019 to 31 March 2022 (already closed)
  - Stamp duty value of property ≤ ₹45 lakh
  - Loan from financial institution (bank/HFC)
  Note: This section's benefit period has ended for new loans. Check if your loan qualifies.

COMBINED HOME LOAN TAX BENEFIT (OLD REGIME)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Principal repayment (80C):    ₹1,50,000
Interest paid (24b):          ₹2,00,000
                               ─────────
Total deduction:               ₹3,50,000
Tax saved at 30% slab:         ₹1,05,000 + cess = ₹1,09,200

JOINT HOME LOAN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Both co-borrowers can claim separately:
  Co-borrower 1: ₹1.5L principal (80C) + ₹2L interest (24b)
  Co-borrower 2: ₹1.5L principal (80C) + ₹2L interest (24b)
  Combined: ₹7,00,000 deduction per year for the couple

Requirements:
  - Both must be co-owners of the property
  - Both must be co-borrowers on the loan
  - Deduction in proportion to ownership share

HOME LOAN vs NEW REGIME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
In New Tax Regime: NO deduction for home loan interest under 24b
Home loan principal under 80C: NOT available in new regime
This is a major factor pushing high home loan payers toward old regime.

Rule of thumb: If annual home loan interest > ₹1.5L, old regime is likely better.
