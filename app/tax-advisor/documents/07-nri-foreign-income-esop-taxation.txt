NRI TAXATION, FOREIGN ASSETS, DTAA, AND ESOP TAX GUIDE
======================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 6, Section 90/91 (DTAA), Section 195 | FY 2026-27

1. RESIDENTIAL STATUS RULES (SECTION 6)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
You are an INDIAN RESIDENT if:
  1. You are in India for 182 days or more in the financial year, OR
  2. You are in India for 60 days or more in the FY AND 365 days or more in the preceding 4 years.
  • Exception for Indian citizens leaving India for employment abroad: 60-day rule replaced with 182 days.
  • Deemed Resident (Section 6(1A)): Indian citizen with total Indian income > ₹15L and not liable to tax in any other country.

2. NRI TAXABILITY & DOUBLE TAXATION RELIEF (DTAA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Taxable Income for NRI: Only income accruing, arising, or received in India (NRO interest, Indian rental income, capital gains in India).
• Foreign Income: 100% EXEMPT from Indian tax for NRIs.
• DTAA Relief (Section 90/91): If tax is paid in foreign country (e.g. US, UK, UAE, Singapore), claim tax credit in India by filing FORM 67 before filing ITR.

3. EMPLOYEE STOCK OPTIONS (ESOPs / RSUs) TAXATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ESOPs are taxed at TWO STAGES:

STAGE 1: AT THE TIME OF EXERCISE (Perquisite Tax)
  • Taxable Amount = (Fair Market Value on exercise date - Exercise Price) * Number of Shares.
  • Taxed as Perquisite under "Income from Salary" at applicable slab rate.
  • Employer deducts TDS on perquisite value.

STAGE 2: AT THE TIME OF SALE (Capital Gains Tax)
  • Taxable Amount = Sale Price - Fair Market Value on exercise date.
  • Taxed as STCG or LTCG depending on holding period from exercise date.
  • Foreign RSUs / US Shares: Holding period > 24 months = LTCG (12.5% without indexation).
