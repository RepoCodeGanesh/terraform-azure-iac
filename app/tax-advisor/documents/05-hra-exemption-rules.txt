HRA EXEMPTION — HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE TAX RULES
================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 10(13A) read with Rule 2A
AVAILABILITY: OLD TAX REGIME ONLY (HRA exemption not available in new regime)

WHAT IS HRA?
HRA (House Rent Allowance) is a salary component paid by employers.
The amount exempt from tax is calculated as the MINIMUM of three values.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HRA EXEMPTION FORMULA — MINIMUM OF THESE THREE:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Actual HRA received from employer
2. 50% of (Basic + DA) — for metro cities
   40% of (Basic + DA) — for non-metro cities
3. Actual rent paid MINUS 10% of (Basic + DA)

METRO CITIES (50% rule applies):
  Mumbai, Delhi (including NCR), Kolkata, Chennai

NON-METRO CITIES (40% rule applies):
  Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, and all other cities

WORKED EXAMPLE — METRO CITY (Mumbai)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Monthly figures:
  Basic salary:    ₹50,000/month → ₹6,00,000/year
  HRA received:    ₹20,000/month → ₹2,40,000/year
  Rent paid:       ₹22,000/month → ₹2,64,000/year
  City: Mumbai (metro)

Calculation (annual):
  1. Actual HRA received:            ₹2,40,000
  2. 50% of Basic:                   ₹3,00,000
  3. Rent paid - 10% of Basic:       ₹2,64,000 - ₹60,000 = ₹2,04,000

  HRA Exempt = MINIMUM = ₹2,04,000
  Taxable HRA = ₹2,40,000 - ₹2,04,000 = ₹36,000

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WORKED EXAMPLE — NON-METRO (Bangalore)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Monthly figures:
  Basic salary:    ₹60,000/month → ₹7,20,000/year
  HRA received:    ₹24,000/month → ₹2,88,000/year
  Rent paid:       ₹28,000/month → ₹3,36,000/year
  City: Bangalore (non-metro — 40%)

Calculation (annual):
  1. Actual HRA received:            ₹2,88,000
  2. 40% of Basic:                   ₹2,88,000
  3. Rent paid - 10% of Basic:       ₹3,36,000 - ₹72,000 = ₹2,64,000

  HRA Exempt = MINIMUM = ₹2,64,000
  Taxable HRA = ₹2,88,000 - ₹2,64,000 = ₹24,000

IMPORTANT CONDITIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. MUST be actually paying rent — exemption not available if living in own house
2. Rent receipts/agreement required as proof
3. If annual rent > ₹1,00,000: landlord's PAN mandatory
4. Cannot claim HRA exemption AND home loan deduction for same property/city
   (But possible if renting in one city, property in another city)
5. Rent paid to parents is valid — must be genuine, proper rent agreement needed
   Landlord parent must declare rent as income in their ITR

SECTION 80GG — HRA FOR THOSE NOT RECEIVING HRA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For salaried employees NOT receiving HRA component OR self-employed:
  Deduction: MINIMUM of —
  1. Actual rent paid - 10% of adjusted gross income
  2. ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000 per year)
  3. 25% of adjusted gross income

Conditions: Must not own residential property anywhere in India.

TIPS:
- Increase basic salary component in your CTC to maximize HRA exemption
- Keep all rent receipts and rental agreement
- If staying with parents, create proper rent agreement and ensure parents declare income
- For freelancers/self-employed: use Section 80GG if you pay rent
