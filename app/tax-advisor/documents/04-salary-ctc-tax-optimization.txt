2. REGIME-SPECIFIC CTC RESTRUCTURING RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
IF TAXPAYER OPTS FOR NEW TAX REGIME (DEFAULT FOR FY 2026-27):
  • STRATEGY 1: EMPLOYER NPS CONTRIBUTION (SECTION 80CCD(2)) — THE MOST POWERFUL SAVING!
    - Shift up to 14% of Basic Salary from Special Allowance into Employer NPS.
    - Example: Salary ₹22 Lakhs, Basic ₹9 Lakhs.
      14% of ₹9L = ₹1,26,000 Employer NPS contribution.
      Tax Saved = ₹1,26,000 × 31.2% = ₹39,312 per year (₹3,276/month extra take-home pay)!
  • STRATEGY 2: OFFICIAL EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS (SECTION 10(14))
    - Telephone / Mobile / Broadband reimbursement (actual bills).
    - Fuel & Driver Allowance for official vehicle usage (Rule 3(2)).
    - Professional Certifications / Books / Periodicals reimbursement.
  • STRATEGY 2: FOOD COUPONS / MEAL CARDS (RULE 15(5)(a) - INCOME TAX RULES 2026)
    - Convert up to ₹4,400/month (₹1,05,600/year) from Special Allowance to Pluxee/Sodexo/Zeta digital food cards.
    - Exemption limit increased from ₹50 to ₹200 per meal (up to 2 meals/working day).
    - 100% Tax-Exempt under BOTH New (Section 115BAC) and Old Tax Regimes for FY 2026-27!
    - Tax Saved = ₹1,05,600 × 31.2% = ₹32,947 per year (₹2,746/month extra take-home pay)!

IF TAXPAYER OPTS FOR OLD TAX REGIME:
  • STRATEGY 1: EMPLOYER NPS (80CCD(2)) + SELF NPS (80CCD(1B))
    - Claim 14% Employer NPS (₹1,26,000) PLUS ₹50,000 Additional Self NPS under 80CCD(1B).
  • STRATEGY 2: HRA & TELEPHONE REIMBURSEMENT
    - Optimize Basic Salary to 40%-50% of CTC to maximize HRA exemption and PF compliance.
