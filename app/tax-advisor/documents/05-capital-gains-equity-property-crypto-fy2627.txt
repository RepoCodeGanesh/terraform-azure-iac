STATUTORY CAPITAL GAINS TAX LAWS: EQUITY, PROPERTY, GOLD & CRYPTO/VDA
=======================================================================

STATUTORY AUTHORITIES: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 111A, Section 112A, Section 112, Section 115BBH, Section 194S | FY 2026-27

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. LISTED SECURITIES & EQUITY MUTUAL FUNDS (STCG & LTCG)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOLDING PERIOD CRITERIA:
  • Short-Term Capital Asset: Held for <= 12 months.
  • Long-Term Capital Asset: Held for > 12 months.

STATUTORY TAX RATES (APPLICABLE FROM 23 JULY 2024 ONWARDS):
  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG - Section 111A): Flat 20% + 4% Cess (previously 15%).
  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG - Section 112A): Flat 12.5% + 4% Cess (previously 10%).
  • Annual LTCG Exemption Limit: First ₹1,25,000 of aggregate LTCG across listed equity shares and equity mutual funds is 100% EXEMPT from tax every financial year (Section 112A(2)).
  • Indexation Benefit: Prohibited for equity shares and equity mutual funds.
  • Securities Transaction Tax (STT): Must be paid on both buy and sell transactions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. IMMOVABLE PROPERTY / REAL ESTATE TAXATION & GRANDFATHERING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOLDING PERIOD CRITERIA:
  • Short-Term Capital Asset: Held for <= 24 months.
  • Long-Term Capital Asset: Held for > 24 months.

STATUTORY TAX RATES & GRANDFATHERING PROVISION (FINANCE ACT 2024/2025):
  • STCG: Taxed at applicable slab rates under Old or New Regime.
  • LTCG (Section 112): Flat 12.5% without indexation.
  • GRANDFATHERING CHOICE FOR ASSETS ACQUIRED BEFORE 23 JULY 2024:
    Resident Individuals and HUFs selling property acquired before 23 July 2024 can compute tax under TWO options and pay whichever is LOWER:
      Option 1: 12.5% tax without indexation benefit.
      Option 2: 20% tax with full CII indexation benefit up to FY 2024-25.

EXEMPTIONS TO ZERO OUT PROPERTY LTCG TAX:
  1. Section 54 Exemption:
     • Reinvest LTCG in purchasing 1 residential house in India within 1 yr before or 2 yrs after sale date (or construct within 3 yrs).
     • Statutory Cap: Maximum Section 54 exemption capped at ₹10 Crore.
  2. Section 54EC Exemption:
     • Invest LTCG in 5-year redeemable capital gains bonds issued by NHAI, REC, PFC, or IRFC within 6 months of sale.
     • Statutory Cap: Maximum investment capped at ₹50,000,000 (₹50 Lakhs) per financial year.

3. DEBT MUTUAL FUNDS & PHYSICAL GOLD
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Specified Mutual Funds (>65% Debt - Section 50AA): 100% Taxed as STCG at applicable slab rates regardless of holding period!
• Physical Gold & Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB): STCG <= 24 months (slab rates), LTCG > 24 months (12.5% without indexation).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. VIRTUAL DIGITAL ASSETS (VDA / CRYPTO / NFTS) — SECTION 115BBH & 194S
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Flat Tax Rate: 30% tax on gains + 4% cess under Section 115BBH.
• TDS under Section 194S: 1% TDS deducted by exchange on transfer of VDA if aggregate transaction exceeds ₹50,000/yr (₹10,000 for non-specified persons).
• PROHIBITION ON LOSS SET-OFF: Loss from one crypto asset CANNOT be set off against gains from another crypto asset or any other income head!
• PROHIBITION ON EXPENSES: No deduction allowed except cost of acquisition. Mining costs, electricity, internet, and transfer fees cannot be deducted.
