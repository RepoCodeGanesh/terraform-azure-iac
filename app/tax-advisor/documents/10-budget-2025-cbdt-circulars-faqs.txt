1. UNION BUDGET 2025 (FINANCE ACT 2025) KEY TAX AMENDMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. ENHANCED SECTION 87A REBATE TO ₹60,000 UNDER NEW REGIME:
   Section 87A tax rebate limit raised to ₹60,000 under Section 115BAC. Net taxable income up to ₹12,00,000 incurs ZERO tax.
   Salaried gross income up to ₹12,75,000 is 100% tax-free after ₹75,000 Standard Deduction.

2. RESTRUCTURED NEW REGIME SLABS (7-TIER STRUCTURE):
   Slabs expanded: Up to 4L (0%), 4L-8L (5%), 8L-12L (10%), 12L-16L (15%), 16L-20L (20%), 20L-24L (25%), >24L (30%).

3. 14% UNIFORM EMPLOYER NPS CONTRIBUTION (SECTION 80CCD(2)):
   Section 80CCD(2) employer NPS limit increased to 14% of Basic+DA for private sector employees (matching govt employees). Works in BOTH New & Old regimes.

4. REVISED CAPITAL GAINS RATES:
   LTCG on listed equity increased to 12.5% with ₹1.25L annual exemption. STCG on listed equity increased to 20%.

2. ADVANCED TAX ADVISOR FAQS & PRACTICAL GUIDANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: Switched jobs mid-financial year. How to avoid tax demand notice?
A: Collect Form 16 from BOTH previous and new employers. Submit Form 12B to new employer showing income and TDS from previous job. If not submitted, both employers grant standard deduction and 0-5% slabs, causing massive tax shortfall under Section 234B/234C when filing ITR!

Q: Can I claim HRA exemption and Home Loan interest deduction together?
A: YES. Under CBDT rules, if you reside in rented premises in your work city and own property in your home city (or under-construction property), you can claim Section 10(13A) HRA exemption AND Section 24(b) ₹2,00,000 home loan interest deduction simultaneously.

Q: Are Sodexo / Pluxee food cards tax-exempt in New Regime?
A: NO. Under Section 115BAC, Rule 3(7)(ix) food coupons / meal vouchers provided via cards are non-exempt perquisites (100% taxable). They are exempt ONLY under the Old Tax Regime up to ₹50/meal (₹26,400/year). Food vouchers MUST NOT be recommended for New Regime tax saving!

Q: What is the treatment of employer NPS (80CCD(2)) in New Regime?
A: Employer NPS contribution under Section 80CCD(2) is 100% EXEMPT under BOTH New and Old regimes up to 14% of Basic Salary + DA. It is the single most effective legal tax reduction strategy under the New Regime!
