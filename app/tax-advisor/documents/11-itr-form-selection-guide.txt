ITR FORM SELECTION GUIDE — WHICH ITR TO FILE
=============================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Department, India | FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ITR-1 (SAHAJ) — Simplest Form
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO CAN FILE:
  ✅ Resident individuals only (not HUF, not NRI)
  ✅ Total income up to ₹50 lakh
  ✅ Income from: Salary/Pension + One House Property + Other Sources (interest, dividends)
  ✅ Agricultural income up to ₹5,000

WHO CANNOT FILE ITR-1:
  ❌ Capital gains (LTCG or STCG) — even LTCG up to ₹1.25L (exempt) disqualifies
  ❌ Business or professional income
  ❌ More than one house property
  ❌ Director of a company
  ❌ Holds unlisted shares
  ❌ Foreign assets or foreign income
  ❌ TDS deducted under Section 194N (cash withdrawal)
  ❌ Deferred ESOP tax
  ❌ Total income above ₹50L

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ITR-2 — For Salaried with Capital Gains / Multiple Properties
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO SHOULD FILE:
  ✅ Individuals and HUF
  ✅ Capital gains (LTCG/STCG from shares, MF, property, gold)
  ✅ Multiple house properties
  ✅ Income above ₹50 lakh
  ✅ Foreign income or assets
  ✅ Director in a company
  ✅ Holds unlisted equity shares
  ✅ NRI (with Indian income)
  ✅ ESOP taxation

WHO CANNOT FILE ITR-2:
  ❌ Business or professional income (turnover from any business)
  ❌ Partnership firm income

WHEN SALARIED PERSON USES ITR-2:
  - Sold any mutual funds / stocks during the year (even at a loss)
  - Has rental income from second property
  - Income > ₹50L
  - Has foreign assets (even zero income)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ITR-3 — Business/Professional Income + Capital Gains
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO SHOULD FILE:
  ✅ Business income (proprietary business)
  ✅ Professional income (doctors, lawyers, CAs, architects, freelancers — if not opting 44ADA)
  ✅ Partner's salary/interest from partnership firm
  ✅ F&O trading income
  ✅ Intraday equity trading (speculative business income)
  ✅ Can also include salary, capital gains, house property

NOTE: F&O losses are business losses — file ITR-3 to carry forward

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ITR-4 (SUGAM) — Presumptive Taxation
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHO SHOULD FILE:
  ✅ Small business opting Section 44AD (presumptive income = 8% of turnover, 6% if digital)
  ✅ Professionals opting Section 44ADA (50% of receipts = income)
    — Doctors, CAs, lawyers, engineers, architects, freelancers
    — Annual receipts up to ₹75 lakh (Budget 2025 enhanced for those with ≥50% digital receipts)
  ✅ Transport business Section 44AE

WHO CANNOT FILE ITR-4:
  ❌ Capital gains
  ❌ More than one house property with losses
  ❌ Income above ₹50L
  ❌ Foreign income/assets
  ❌ Director in company

QUICK DECISION TREE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q1: Do you have business/professional income?
  YES → ITR-3 (if not presumptive) or ITR-4 (if presumptive)
  NO  → Continue

Q2: Do you have capital gains (any amount)?
  YES → ITR-2
  NO  → Continue

Q3: Do you have income > ₹50L or foreign assets or 2+ properties?
  YES → ITR-2
  NO  → ITR-1

IMPORTANT DEADLINES (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Original filing deadline:     31 July 2026 (for non-audit cases)
  Audit cases:                  31 October 2026
  Belated return:               31 December 2026
  Revised return:               31 December 2026
  
  Penalty for late filing:
    Income < ₹5L: ₹1,000
    Income > ₹5L: ₹5,000 (if filed after 31 July but before 31 Dec)
    Plus: Interest @ 1%/month on unpaid tax (Section 234A)

ITR FILING MODES:
  - Income Tax e-Filing portal: www.incometax.gov.in
  - Authorized e-Return Intermediaries (ERIs) — CAs, tax portals
  - Offline utility available for ITR-2, ITR-3
