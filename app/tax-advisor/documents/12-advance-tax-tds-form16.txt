ADVANCE TAX, TDS, FORM 16, AND REFUND GUIDE
============================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 207-219 (Advance Tax), Section 192 (TDS on Salary)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ADVANCE TAX — WHO MUST PAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Advance tax must be paid if estimated tax liability > ₹10,000 in a financial year.
Applicable if you have income beyond salary (capital gains, freelance, rent, etc.)
Salaried employees: TDS by employer usually covers tax; advance tax only if additional income.

ADVANCE TAX DUE DATES (FY 2026-27):
  15 June 2026:     15% of estimated annual tax
  15 September 2026: 45% of estimated annual tax (cumulative)
  15 December 2026:  75% of estimated annual tax (cumulative)
  15 March 2027:    100% of estimated annual tax

INTEREST FOR NON-PAYMENT (Section 234B and 234C):
  234B: 1% per month if advance tax paid < 90% of assessed tax
  234C: 1% per month for deferment of each installment
  These interest charges are added when filing ITR.

FORM 16 — TDS CERTIFICATE FOR SALARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Issued by employer by 15 June after the FY ends.
Two parts:
  PART A: TDS amount deducted quarter-wise with employer TAN
  PART B: Detailed salary breakup and deductions claimed
           - Gross salary
           - Exemptions (HRA, LTA, etc.)
           - Standard deduction
           - Section 80C, 80D, etc.
           - Net taxable income
           - Total TDS deducted

TAX REFUND PROCESS:
  Refund arises when: TDS deducted > actual tax liability
  File ITR with correct bank account (pre-validated)
  e-Verify return within 30 days
  Income Tax Dept processes and issues refund
