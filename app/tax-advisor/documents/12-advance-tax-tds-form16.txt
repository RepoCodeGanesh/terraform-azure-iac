ADVANCE TAX, TDS, FORM 16, AND REFUND GUIDE
============================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Sections 207-219 (Advance Tax), Section 192 (TDS on Salary)

ADVANCE TAX — WHO MUST PAY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Advance tax must be paid if estimated tax liability > ₹10,000 in a financial year.
Applicable if you have income beyond salary (capital gains, freelance, rent, etc.)
Salaried employees: TDS by employer usually covers tax; advance tax only if additional income.

ADVANCE TAX DUE DATES (FY 2025-26):
  15 June 2025:     15% of estimated annual tax
  15 September 2025: 45% of estimated annual tax (cumulative)
  15 December 2025:  75% of estimated annual tax (cumulative)
  15 March 2026:    100% of estimated annual tax

INTEREST FOR NON-PAYMENT (Section 234B and 234C):
  234B: 1% per month if advance tax paid < 90% of assessed tax
  234C: 1% per month for deferment of each installment
  These interest charges are added when filing ITR.

TDS ON SALARY (Section 192)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Employer deducts TDS monthly based on projected annual income and declared investments.

How it works:
  1. Declare regime choice (old/new) to employer at start of financial year
  2. Submit investment declaration Form 12BB (HRA receipts, 80C proofs, etc.)
  3. Employer calculates estimated annual tax and divides by 12 months
  4. Each month, TDS = estimated monthly tax
  5. At year end, submit actual proof of investments — employer adjusts final TDS

FORM 12BB — Investment Declaration:
  Submit to employer at start of year with:
  - HRA: Rent receipts + landlord PAN (if rent > ₹1L/year)
  - Section 80C: LIC premium, ELSS, PPF passbook, EPF statement, etc.
  - Section 80D: Health insurance premium receipts
  - Home loan: Interest certificate from bank
  - LTA: Travel bills
  Submit proofs before March end for final TDS computation

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FORM 16 — TDS CERTIFICATE FOR SALARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Issued by employer by 15 June after the FY ends.
Two parts:
  PART A: TDS amount deducted quarter-wise with employer TAN
  PART B: Detailed salary breakup and deductions claimed
           - Gross salary
           - Exemptions (HRA, LTA, etc.)
           - Standard deduction
           - Section 80C, 80D, etc.
           - Net taxable income
           - Total TDS deducted

How to use Form 16 for ITR:
  - Pre-fill ITR using AIS/Form 26AS data
  - Cross-check Form 16 Part B with your own calculations
  - If TDS > actual tax: Refund due
  - If TDS < actual tax: Pay self-assessment tax before filing

FORM 26AS — TAX CREDIT STATEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Available at: www.incometax.gov.in → e-File → Income Tax Returns → View Form 26AS
Shows:
  - TDS deducted by employer, banks, clients
  - Advance tax paid
  - Self-assessment tax paid
  - TCS collected
  - Any high-value transactions

Annual Information Statement (AIS):
  More comprehensive than 26AS; includes MF transactions, interest, dividends.
  Available on income tax portal.
  Useful for accurate capital gains reporting.

TAX REFUND PROCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Refund arises when: TDS deducted > actual tax liability

Steps:
  1. File ITR with correct bank account (pre-validated, same PAN)
  2. e-Verify return within 30 days (Aadhaar OTP / net banking / EVC)
  3. Income Tax Dept processes and issues refund to bank account
  4. Timeline: Usually 2-8 weeks after e-verification
  5. Refund interest: 6% per year if refund > 10% of assessed tax (Section 244A)

Track refund: www.incometax.gov.in → Services → Refund Reissue / Status
Or: NSDL portal → tin.tin.nsdl.com/oltas/refundstatuslogin.html

COMMON REASONS FOR EXCESS TDS:
  - Employer didn't account for declared investments
  - Switched from old to new regime at last minute
  - Had capital loss to set off against gains
  - Left job mid-year (excess TDS due to lower total income)
  - Bank deducted TDS on FD interest but total income below taxable limit

HOW TO CLAIM TDS REFUND ON FD INTEREST (Form 15G/15H):
  Form 15G: Submit to bank if income < basic exemption limit (non-senior)
  Form 15H: Submit to bank if income < basic exemption limit (senior citizen 60+)
  Effect: Bank will NOT deduct TDS on FD interest
  Must be submitted at start of each financial year
