# Salary Structure & CTC Tax Optimisation Master Guide

## 1. Taxability Matrix of Salary Components & Perquisites

| Salary Component | Old Tax Regime Status | New Tax Regime Status (Sec 115BAC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Salary** | 100% Taxable | 100% Taxable |
| **Dearness Allowance (DA)** | 100% Taxable | 100% Taxable |
| **House Rent Allowance (HRA)** | Exempt u/s 10(13A) (Rule 2A formula) | 100% Taxable (No Exemption) |
| **Special Allowance** | 100% Taxable | 100% Taxable |
| **Standard Deduction** | ₹50,000 u/s 16(ia) | **₹75,000 u/s 16(ia)** |
| **Employer NPS (80CCD(2))** | Exempt up to 10% Basic+DA (Pvt) / 14% (Govt) | **Exempt up to 14% Basic+DA (Pvt & Govt)** |
| **Food Cards / Vouchers** | Exempt up to ₹200/meal (₹1,05,600/yr) | **Exempt up to ₹200/meal (₹1,05,600/yr) u/r 15(5)(a)** |
| **Telephone / Broadband** | Exempt against actual bills | Exempt against actual bills |
| **L&D / Professional Cert.** | Exempt against actual bills | Exempt against actual bills |
| **Conveyance Allowance** | Exempt u/s 10(14) for official work | Exempt u/s 10(14) for official work |
| **Leave Travel Allowance (LTA)** | Exempt 2 journeys in 4 yrs u/s 10(5) | 100% Taxable |

## 2. Regime-Specific CTC Restructuring Rules

### If Taxpayer Opts for New Tax Regime (Default for FY 2026-27)

#### Strategy 1: Employer NPS Contribution (Section 80CCD(2)) — The Most Powerful Saving!
* Shift up to **14% of Basic Salary** from Special Allowance into Employer NPS.
* *Example*: Salary ₹22 Lakhs, Basic ₹9 Lakhs.
  - 14% of ₹9L = **₹1,26,000 Employer NPS contribution**.
  - **Tax Saved** = ₹1,26,000 $\times$ 31.2% = **₹39,312 per year** (₹3,276/month extra take-home pay)!

#### Strategy 2: Official Expense Reimbursements (Permitted in New Regime)
* **Telephone / Mobile / Broadband Reimbursement**: 100% EXEMPT against actual bills (Non-taxable perquisite u/r 3(7)(ix) / Sec 17(2)).
* **Official Conveyance & Travel on Tour (Sec 10(14)(i))**: 100% EXEMPT against actual official duties supported by bills/logbook.
* **Professional Certifications / Books Reimbursement**: 100% EXEMPT against actual invoices for job skills.

> [!CAUTION]
> Fixed cash allowances without bill proof are **100% TAXABLE** in New Regime. Only actual bill reimbursements qualify!

#### Strategy 3: Food Coupons / Meal Cards (Rule 15(5)(a) - Income Tax Rules 2026)
* **Maximum Statutory Exemption**: **₹8,800/month** (**₹1,05,600/year**).
* Calculated as ₹200/meal $\times$ 2 meals/day $\times$ 22 working days.
* 100% Tax-Exempt under **BOTH** New (Section 115BAC) and Old Tax Regimes for FY 2026-27!
* **Tax Saved** = ₹1,05,600 $\times$ 31.2% = **₹32,947 per year** (₹2,746/month extra take-home pay)!

### If Taxpayer Opts for Old Tax Regime

#### Strategy 1: Employer NPS (80CCD(2)) + Self NPS (80CCD(1B))
* Claim 14% Employer NPS (₹1,26,000) **PLUS** ₹50,000 Additional Self NPS under 80CCD(1B).

#### Strategy 2: HRA & Telephone Reimbursement
* Optimize Basic Salary to 40%–50% of CTC to maximize HRA exemption and PF compliance.
