FREQUENTLY ASKED TAX QUESTIONS — FILING GUIDE
===============================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Department India, CBDT FAQs, Finance Act 2025

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: I switched jobs mid-year. How do I file ITR?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: Collect Form 16 from BOTH employers. Add both salaries.
Submit Form 12B to new employer showing income from previous employer so
they can deduct correct TDS. File ITR-1 or ITR-2 combining both incomes.
Often results in excess TDS from first employer → refund.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: Can I claim both HRA and home loan?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: YES, if property and rented residence are in different cities.
Example: Property in Pune (home loan), working and renting in Mumbai.
Both HRA exemption and Section 24b interest deduction can be claimed.
If in same city: Only one can be claimed (either HRA or home loan, whichever is higher).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: What if I missed the ITR deadline?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: File a belated return by 31 December of the assessment year.
Penalty: ₹5,000 (₹1,000 if income < ₹5L). Plus interest 234A.
Belated returns cannot carry forward most losses (except house property loss).
Still file even if late — penalties are less costly than non-filing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: Is it mandatory to file ITR if TDS was already deducted?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: Filing is mandatory if:
  - Total income exceeds basic exemption limit (₹2.5L old / ₹3L new)
  - You want to claim TDS refund
  - You have foreign assets or foreign income
  - Turnover from business > ₹60L
  - TDS deducted > ₹25,000 (or ₹50K for senior citizens) during the year
  - Electricity consumption > 1 lakh units in a year
Even if no tax is due, filing is advisable for visa purposes, loan applications.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: How do I know which tax regime is better for me?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: Calculate tax under both regimes:
  NEW REGIME: Apply slabs, no deductions (except employer NPS, standard deduction)
  OLD REGIME: Apply slabs after all deductions (80C, 80D, HRA, home loan, etc.)
  
  Old regime is better if total deductions > the break-even point:
    Salary ₹10L: Deductions need to exceed ~₹2.5L
    Salary ₹15L: Deductions need to exceed ~₹3.75L
    Salary ₹20L: Deductions need to exceed ~₹4.25L
  
  Use TaxBot's Regime Comparator for exact calculation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: My employer deducted tax under old regime but I want new regime. What to do?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: When filing ITR, choose new regime. Recalculate tax. If you've overpaid TDS,
claim refund in ITR. The regime in ITR supersedes employer's TDS calculation.
Inform employer next year at start of FY to deduct TDS under new regime.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: I have ESOP from my company. How is it taxed?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: Two tax events:
  1. Exercise: FMV - Exercise Price = Perquisite income (taxable as salary, TDS by employer)
  2. Sale: Sale price - FMV at exercise = Capital gains
     - Listed shares: LTCG (12.5% if 12+ months), STCG (20% if <12 months)
     - Unlisted shares: LTCG (12.5% if 24+ months), STCG (slab if <24 months)
  File ITR-2 for ESOP. Employer deducts TDS on perquisite at time of exercise.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Q: I have an FD with ₹5 lakh. Is interest taxable?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: Yes, FD interest is 100% taxable at your slab rate (not 30% flat).
TDS: Bank deducts 10% TDS if interest > ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).
To avoid TDS: Submit Form 15G (non-senior) or 15H (senior) if income is below exemption.
Report interest income in ITR even if Form 15G submitted.
Senior citizens: Deduct up to ₹50,000 under Section 80TTB.

Q: How are freelance/consulting income taxed?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A: If annual receipts < ₹75 lakh: Use Section 44ADA presumptive taxation.
   50% of receipts = professional income (no books of accounts needed).
   Example: Receipts ₹20L → Taxable income = ₹10L (50%)
   TDS: Clients deduct 10% TDS on payments > ₹30,000 per professional (Section 194J).
   File ITR-4 if using presumptive taxation. ITR-3 if maintaining accounts.
   Can avail all deductions (80C, 80D, etc.) in old regime.
   Cannot opt for 44ADA in new regime — declare actual income.
