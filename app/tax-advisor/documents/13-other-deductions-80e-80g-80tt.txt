OTHER IMPORTANT DEDUCTIONS — 80E, 80G, 80GG, 80TTA, 80TTB
===========================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961 | AVAILABILITY: OLD TAX REGIME ONLY

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80E — EDUCATION LOAN INTEREST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: 100% of interest paid on education loan (NO limit on amount)
Duration: 8 consecutive years from start of repayment
Eligible: Self, spouse, children, or student for whom you are legal guardian

Course eligible: Higher education (any field) in India or abroad
Loan from: Bank, financial institution, or approved charitable institution
NOT eligible: Loan from relatives or employers

Example:
  Education loan interest paid: ₹1,20,000/year
  Deduction: Full ₹1,20,000
  Tax saving at 30%: ₹36,000

Note: Principal repayment is NOT deductible (only interest)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80G — DONATIONS TO CHARITABLE ORGANISATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: 50% or 100% of donation depending on organisation
Payment: Cheque/DD/online only for amounts > ₹2,000 (cash donations > ₹2K not eligible)

100% deduction (no qualifying limit):
  - PM National Relief Fund
  - PM CARES Fund
  - National Defence Fund
  - CM Relief Fund (during natural calamity)

100% deduction (subject to qualifying limit of 10% of adjusted gross total income):
  - Donations to approved scientific research institutions

50% deduction (subject to 10% qualifying limit):
  - Most registered NGOs and charitable trusts
  - Indian Olympic Association

How to claim:
  - Ensure organisation has 80G certificate (check on IT portal)
  - Obtain receipt with donor's name, amount, PAN of organisation
  - Claim in ITR; donee must file Form 10BD

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80GG — RENT DEDUCTION (No HRA in salary)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For taxpayers NOT receiving HRA (self-employed, or salary without HRA component)
Deduction: MINIMUM of:
  1. Rent paid - 10% of total income
  2. ₹5,000 per month (₹60,000/year)
  3. 25% of total income

Conditions:
  - No residential property owned by self, spouse, minor child
  - File Form 10BA declaration to claim this

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80TTA — INTEREST ON SAVINGS ACCOUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: Up to ₹10,000 on interest earned from savings bank accounts
Eligible: Savings account in bank, post office, co-operative society
NOT eligible: Fixed deposits, recurring deposits, time deposits

For taxpayers below 60 years
Senior citizens use Section 80TTB instead (higher limit)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80TTB — INTEREST FOR SENIOR CITIZENS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction: Up to ₹50,000 on interest income
Eligible: Senior citizens (60+ years)
Covers: Savings account + Fixed deposits + Recurring deposits + Time deposits
This replaces Section 80TTA for senior citizens (cannot claim both)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80U — DISABILITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For differently-abled taxpayers (self):
  40-80% disability:  ₹75,000 deduction
  Severe (80%+):      ₹1,25,000 deduction
Requires disability certificate from government medical authority

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80DD — DISABLED DEPENDENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For expenses on dependent with disability:
  40-80% disability:  ₹75,000 deduction
  Severe (80%+):      ₹1,25,000 deduction
Covers: Medical treatment, nursing, training, rehabilitation
Dependent: Spouse, children, parents, siblings

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80DDB — SPECIFIED DISEASE TREATMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
For medical treatment of specified diseases (cancer, AIDS, Parkinson's, etc.):
  Below 60 years:     ₹40,000 deduction
  Senior citizen 60+: ₹1,00,000 deduction
Requires prescription from specialist doctor
Reduces by amount received from insurance/employer
