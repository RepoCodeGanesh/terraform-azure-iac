SENIOR CITIZENS & PENSIONERS STATUTORY TAX GUIDE
===================================================

STATUTORY AUTHORITIES: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 80TTB, Section 194P, Section 194A, Section 57(iia) | FY 2026-27

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. AGE CLASSIFICATION & BASIC EXEMPTION LIMITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
OLD TAX REGIME EXEMPTION LIMITS:
  • Senior Citizen (Individual Resident aged 60 to 79 years): Basic Exemption Limit = ₹3,00,000.
  • Super Senior Citizen (Individual Resident aged 80 years or above): Basic Exemption Limit = ₹5,00,000.

NEW TAX REGIME (SECTION 115BAC):
  • Basic Exemption Limit = ₹4,00,000 for ALL age groups.
  • Combined with Section 87A rebate (₹60,000 limit), zero tax payable up to ₹12,00,000 net income.

2. SPECIAL STATUTORY DEDUCTIONS FOR SENIOR CITIZENS (OLD REGIME)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
SECTION 80TTB — INTEREST INCOME DEDUCTION (UP TO ₹50,000):
  • Deduction up to ₹50,000 per financial year for interest income from Savings Accounts, Fixed Deposits (FD), Recurring Deposits (RD), and Post Office schemes.
  • TDS Threshold (Section 194A): Banks/Post offices CANNOT deduct TDS on FD interest for senior citizens unless total interest exceeds ₹50,000/yr (Submit Form 15H).

SECTION 80D — HEALTH INSURANCE & MEDICAL EXPENDITURE (UP TO ₹50,000):
  • Health insurance premium deduction up to ₹50,000/yr.
  • If health insurance is not available, medical expenditure incurred for senior citizen is deductible up to ₹50,000/yr.

SECTION 80DDB — CRITICAL ILLNESS TREATMENT (UP TO ₹1,00,000):
  • Deduction up to ₹1,00,000 for medical treatment of specified critical ailments (Parkinson's, Cancer, Renal failure).

3. SECTION 194P — EXEMPTION FROM FILING ITR FOR SENIOR CITIZENS 75+
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Senior citizens aged 75 years or above are STATUTORILY EXEMPT from filing ITR if they satisfy all conditions:
  1. Resident individual aged 75 years or above.
  2. Total income consists ONLY of pension income and interest income from the same specified bank.
  3. Declaration in Form 12BBA submitted to specified bank.
  4. Bank calculates total income and deducts full TDS under Section 194P.

4. PENSION TAXABILITY & FAMILY PENSION DEDUCTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Uncommuted (Monthly) Pension: 100% Taxable under "Income from Salaries" for both private and govt employees.
• Commuted (Lump sum) Pension:
  - Govt Employees: 100% EXEMPT under Section 10(10A)(i).
  - Private Employees: Exempt up to 1/3rd (if receiving gratuity) or 1/2 (if not receiving gratuity).
• Family Pension (Section 57(iia)): Received by legal heirs of deceased employee.
  - New Regime: Deduction allowed up to ₹25,000 or 1/3rd of pension (whichever is lower).
  - Old Regime: Deduction allowed up to ₹15,000 or 1/3rd of pension (whichever is lower).
