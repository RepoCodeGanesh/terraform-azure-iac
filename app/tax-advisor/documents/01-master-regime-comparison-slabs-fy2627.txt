MASTER TAX REGIME COMPARISON & SLAB RATES (FY 2026-27 / AY 2027-28)
===================================================================

STATUTORY AUTHORITIES: Income Tax Act 1961, Section 115BAC, Finance Act 2025, CBDT Circulars

1. NEW TAX REGIME (SECTION 115BAC) — DEFAULT TAX REGIME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Under Section 115BAC(1A), the New Tax Regime is the DEFAULT tax regime for all Individuals, HUF, AOP, BOI, and Artificial Juridical Persons for Assessment Year 2027-28 (Financial Year 2026-27).

REVISED SLAB STRUCTURE (FY 2026-27):
  Total Net Income (₹)            Tax Rate (%)
  --------------------------------------------
  Up to ₹4,00,000                0% (Nil)
  ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000         5%
  ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000        10%
  ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000       15%
  ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000       20%
  ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000       25%
  Above ₹24,00,000               30%

STATUTORY ALLOWANCES & DEDUCTIONS PERMITTED IN NEW REGIME:
  1. Standard Deduction under Section 16(ia): ₹75,000 for salaried employees and pensioners.
  2. Employer Contribution to NPS under Section 80CCD(2): Up to 14% of (Basic Salary + Dearness Allowance) for both Private and Government sector employees.
  3. Family Pension Deduction under Section 57(iia): ₹25,000 or 1/3rd of family pension, whichever is lower.
  4. Transport Allowance for Specially-Abled Employees: Under Section 10(14).
  5. Conveyance & Travel Allowance for Official Duties: Under Section 10(14) (against actual official expenses).
  6. Section 87A Rebate: Maximum rebate of ₹60,000 if Net Taxable Income <= ₹12,00,000.
     • Zero-Tax Ceiling: Salaried individuals earning gross income up to ₹12,75,000 pay ₹0 tax after claiming ₹75,000 Standard Deduction!
     • Marginal Relief under 87A: If Net Income slightly exceeds ₹12,00,000, tax payable cannot exceed the income exceeding ₹12,00,000.

DEDUCTIONS & EXEMPTIONS EXPRESSLY PROHIBITED IN NEW REGIME:
  ❌ Section 80C (PPF, EPF, ELSS, LIC, Tuition Fees, Home Loan Principal)
  ❌ Section 80D (Health Insurance Premium & Preventive Health Checkup)
  ❌ Section 80CCD(1B) (Additional ₹50,000 Self NPS Contribution)
  ❌ Section 10(13A) (House Rent Allowance / HRA Exemption)
  ❌ Section 24(b) (Home Loan Interest Deduction on Self-Occupied Property)
  ❌ Section 10(5) (Leave Travel Allowance / LTA Exemption)
  ❌ Rule 3(7)(ix) (Food Coupons / Meal Vouchers / Sodexo Cards)
  ❌ Section 80E (Education Loan Interest)
  ❌ Section 80G / 80GGC (Charitable & Political Donations)
  ❌ Section 80TTA / 80TTB (Savings Account / FD Interest Deductions)

2. OLD TAX REGIME — OPTIONAL REGIME
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Taxpayers must explicitly opt out of Section 115BAC at the time of filing ITR (Form 10-IEA for business income) to use the Old Tax Regime.

SLAB STRUCTURE FOR GENERAL INDIVIDUALS (BELOW 60 YEARS):
  Total Net Income (₹)            Tax Rate (%)
  --------------------------------------------
  Up to ₹2,50,000                0% (Nil)
  ₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000         5%
  ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000        20%
  Above ₹10,00,000               30%

OLD REGIME SECTION 87A REBATE:
  Rebate of 100% of tax up to ₹12,500 if Net Taxable Income <= ₹5,00,000.

3. SURCHARGE & CESS STRUCTURE (FY 2026-27)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Health & Education Cess: Mandatory 4% on aggregate of (Income Tax + Surcharge).

Surcharge Slabs:
  • Total Income > ₹50 Lakhs up to ₹1 Crore:  10%
  • Total Income > ₹1 Crore up to ₹2 Crores: 15%
  • Total Income > ₹2 Crores up to ₹5 Crores: 25%
  • Total Income > ₹5 Crores: 25% under New Regime (Capped) | 37% under Old Regime.
  • Special Cap: Surcharge on Equity LTCG (Sec 112A), STCG (Sec 111A), and Dividend Income is capped at 15%.
