# ITR Form Selection, TDS Rates, Advance Tax, and AIS/26AS Reconciliation

## 1. Statutory ITR Form Selection & Disqualifications

### ITR-1 (SAHAJ)
* **✅ WHO CAN FILE**: Resident Individuals having total income up to **₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs)** from Salary/Pension, **ONE House Property**, and Other Sources (Interest/Dividend). Agricultural income $\le$ ₹5,000.
* **❌ DISQUALIFIED IF**:
  - Capital Gains exist (LTCG / STCG) — even tax-exempt LTCG disqualifies ITR-1!
  - More than one house property.
  - Business or Professional income.
  - Director in a company or holds unlisted equity shares.
  - Foreign assets or foreign income / accounts.
  - Deferred ESOP tax.
  - TDS under Section 194N (cash withdrawals).

### ITR-2
* **✅ WHO MUST FILE**: Salaried individuals with Capital Gains (equity, property, crypto), >1 House Property, Income > ₹50 Lakhs, Foreign Assets/Income, NRIs, ESOPs, Company Directors, Unlisted shares.

### ITR-3
* **✅ WHO MUST FILE**: Individuals having Business/Professional Income (Proprietary business, F&O trading, Intraday equity trading, Partnership firm partner income) along with Capital Gains or Salary.

### ITR-4 (SUGAM)
* **✅ WHO CAN FILE**: Small business opting Section 44AD or Professionals opting Section 44ADA (Presumptive income) up to turnover caps.

## 2. Advance Tax Schedule & Interest Penalties (Section 207-219)

Mandatory if estimated net tax liability (after TDS) exceeds **₹10,000** in a financial year.

### Payment Schedule

| Due Date | Minimum Cumulative Advance Tax Payable |
| :--- | :---: |
| **On or before 15 June** | **15%** of estimated net tax |
| **On or before 15 September** | **45%** of estimated net tax |
| **On or before 15 December** | **75%** of estimated net tax |
| **On or before 15 March** | **100%** of estimated net tax |
| **Presumptive Sec 44AD / 44ADA** | **100% on or before 15 March** (in 1 single installment) |

### Statutory Interest Penalties

* **Section 234B**: **1% per month** for default in payment of advance tax (if paid tax < 90% of assessed tax).
* **Section 234C**: **1% per month** for deferment of advance tax installments.
* **Section 234A**: **1% per month** for late filing of ITR beyond 31 July.
* **Section 234F**: Penalty of **₹5,000** (₹1,000 if income < ₹5L) for belated return filed after 31 July up to 31 Dec.

## 3. AIS, TIS, and Form 26AS Mandatory Reconciliation

Before submitting ITR, taxpayers **MUST** reconcile data across 3 official statements:

1. **Form 26AS**: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS), Tax Collected at Source (TCS), Advance Tax paid.
2. **Annual Information Statement (AIS)**: Comprehensive record of stock transactions, mutual fund buy/sell, dividend income, FD interest, savings bank interest, property transactions, foreign outward remittances.
3. **Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS)**: Aggregated summary derived from AIS.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Discrepancy Rule**: Any mismatch between AIS and ITR will trigger an automated e-Verification notice under Section 133(6) or Section 143(1) from CBDT!
