MASTER GUIDE TO CHAPTER VI-A DEDUCTIONS (80C, 80D, 80CCD, 80E, 80G)
===================================================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, CBDT Guidelines | FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. SECTION 80C — OVERALL CEILING: ₹1,50,000 (OLD REGIME ONLY)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Allowed Investment Instruments:
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF) & Voluntary Provident Fund (VPF)
  • Public Provident Fund (PPF): 15-year lock-in, tax-free interest & maturity (EEE)
  • Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS Mutual Funds): 3-year lock-in, highest return potential
  • National Savings Certificate (NSC) & Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)
  • Tax-Saving Fixed Deposit (5-year bank FD)
  • Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) for girl child
  • Life Insurance Premium (LIC / Term Insurance)
  • Children's School / College Tuition Fees (max 2 children)
  • Principal repayment of Home Loan

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SECTION 80D — HEALTH INSURANCE & PREVENTIVE CHECKUPS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Deduction Limits (Old Regime Only):
  • Self, Spouse & Dependent Children (Below 60 yrs): Up to ₹25,000
  • Self & Family (Senior Citizens 60+ yrs): Up to ₹50,000
  • Parents (Below 60 yrs): Additional ₹25,000
  • Parents (Senior Citizens 60+ yrs): Additional ₹50,000
  • Preventive Health Checkup: ₹5,000 sub-limit included within the above caps.
  • Medical Expenditure for Senior Citizen Parents without health insurance: Up to ₹50,000.
MAXIMUM COMBINED DEDUCTION: ₹1,00,000 (if self and parents are both 60+).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. SECTION 80CCD — NATIONAL PENSION SYSTEM (NPS)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Section 80CCD(1): Self-contribution included within Section 80C ₹1.5L cap.
• Section 80CCD(1B): Additional self-contribution up to ₹50,000 (Exclusive to Old Regime).
• Section 80CCD(2): EMPLOYER NPS CONTRIBUTION — WORKS IN BOTH NEW & OLD REGIMES!
    - Private Sector Employees: Deduction up to 14% of Basic + DA.
    - Central / State Govt Employees: Deduction up to 14% of Basic + DA.
    - No upper monetary limit (subject to annual ₹7.5L combined cap on PF + NPS + Superannuation).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. SECTION 80E — HIGHER EDUCATION LOAN INTEREST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Interest paid on loan taken for higher education (Self, Spouse, Children).
• No monetary limit on deduction amount.
• Available for a maximum of 8 consecutive years starting from the year repayment begins.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
5. SECTION 80G & 80GGC — DONATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• 80G: Charitable donations to notified funds (100% or 50% deduction). Must be paid electronically if > ₹2,000.
• 80GGC: 100% deduction for contributions to registered political parties (Non-cash only).
