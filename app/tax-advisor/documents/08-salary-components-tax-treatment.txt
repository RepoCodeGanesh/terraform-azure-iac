SALARY COMPONENTS AND THEIR TAX TREATMENT
==========================================

SOURCE: Income Tax Act 1961, Various Sections

This document explains how each component of a typical Indian salary slip
is treated for income tax purposes.

FULLY TAXABLE SALARY COMPONENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. BASIC SALARY — 100% Taxable
   - Core of salary, basis for HRA/PF/Gratuity calculations
   - Fully added to taxable income
   - Higher basic = higher EPF, higher HRA exemption, higher gratuity

2. SPECIAL ALLOWANCE / FLEXI ALLOWANCE — 100% Taxable
   - Given as top-up to meet CTC target
   - No exemption whatsoever
   - Most common area to restructure for tax saving
   - Can be converted to: NPS employer contribution, meal vouchers, phone reimbursement

3. PERFORMANCE BONUS / VARIABLE PAY — 100% Taxable
   - Added to income in year of receipt
   - Taxed at applicable slab rate
   - TDS deducted when paid

4. CITY COMPENSATORY ALLOWANCE — 100% Taxable

5. OVERTIME ALLOWANCE — 100% Taxable (for non-government employees)

PARTIALLY EXEMPT COMPONENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6. HRA (HOUSE RENT ALLOWANCE) — Partially Exempt (OLD REGIME)
   - Exempt portion: Minimum of 3 values (see HRA document)
   - New regime: FULLY TAXABLE
   - Common: HRA = 40-50% of basic salary

7. LEAVE TRAVEL ALLOWANCE (LTA) — Exempt twice in 4 years (OLD REGIME)
   - Exemption for domestic travel (air, rail, road) for self and family
   - Only economy class airfare or AC 1st class rail fare
   - Must submit travel tickets/bills
   - Covers travel between home city and destination only
   - New regime: FULLY TAXABLE

8. MEDICAL ALLOWANCE (old policies)
   - Companies still paying this: ₹1,250/month → ₹15,000/year was historically exempt
   - Now: Medical reimbursement is taxable; replaced by standard deduction
   - Standard deduction of ₹50,000 (old regime) / ₹75,000 (new regime) covers this

FULLY EXEMPT COMPONENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

9. EMPLOYER PF CONTRIBUTION — Exempt up to 12% of basic salary
   - Employer's contribution up to 12% of basic+DA: Tax-free
   - Employer contribution exceeding 12%: Taxable
   - Interest on PF: Tax-free (below threshold)
   - PF maturity (after 5 years continuous service): Tax-free

10. EMPLOYER NPS CONTRIBUTION (80CCD(2)) — Exempt up to 14% of basic+DA
    - Available in BOTH old and new regime
    - Up to 14% of (basic + DA) for all employees (Budget 2024 update)
    - This amount is not added to employee's salary for tax purposes

11. GRATUITY (on separation/retirement) — Exempt up to ₹20,00,000
    - Calculated as: (15/26) × last drawn salary × years of service
    - Exempt: Up to ₹20 lakh (as per latest amendments)
    - Payable after 5 years continuous service

12. LEAVE ENCASHMENT (at retirement) — Fully Exempt for Government employees
    - Private employees: Exempt up to ₹25,00,000 (₹25 lakh, enhanced in 2023)
    - During service: Fully taxable if encashed (not at retirement)

13. PROFESSIONAL TAX — Deductible
    - State-levied professional tax (max ₹2,500/year) is deductible
    - Shown as deduction in Form 16

14. MEAL VOUCHERS / SODEXO CARDS
    - Up to ₹50 per meal for 2 meals per working day: Tax-free
    - Approximately: ₹26,400/year tax-free (for 22 working days × 2 meals × ₹50 × 12)

15. TELEPHONE / INTERNET REIMBURSEMENT
    - Actual bills reimbursed: Tax-free (with bills)
    - Typically ₹1,000-2,000/month

HOW TO READ A SALARY SLIP FOR TAX PURPOSES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Gross CTC:                ₹20,00,000
Less: Employer PF:          (₹1,00,000)  [12% of ₹8.33L basic, excluded]
Less: Employer NPS:         (₹1,00,000)  [14% of basic — negotiated]
Fixed Pay (take-home CTC): ₹18,00,000
Less: Employee PF:           (₹72,000)   [80C eligible]
Less: Professional Tax:       (₹2,500)   [deductible]
Taxable Salary before HRA: ₹17,25,500
Less: HRA exempt portion:  (₹2,40,000)  [if in old regime and paying rent]
Less: Standard Deduction:    (₹50,000)   [old regime]
Net Taxable Income:        ₹14,35,500

CTC RESTRUCTURING OPPORTUNITY:
  Convert Special Allowance → Employer NPS (up to 14% basic)
  This reduces taxable salary without reducing actual payout significantly
  Works in BOTH tax regimes
